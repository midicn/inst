童声与变声期

声音的窗口期 —— 每个必然经历又必然失去的声部

相关

人声声部分类音高音色

⭐⭐ 主线：声音的窗口期

「During puberty, the male vocal chords increase by 63%, while the female vocal chords increase only by about half that rate at 34%. Prepubescent male vocal chords grow from an average of 17.35mm to 28.92mm by the time they reach adulthood」.

⭐⭐ 童声是每个人必经、又必然失去的唯一声部——全站唯一一件「会自己变质的乐器」。⭐⭐ 而人类为这个必然失去的声音，造出了音乐史上最黑暗的制度（阉伶）与最温柔的传统（童声合唱团）—— 一端是手术刀，一端是唱诗班。

HS 分类归属
HS 分类人体（气鸣 · 簧振 + 滤波）——童声是同一件乐器的「早期固件」：硬件未长成，先跑起了高音
出身⭐⭐ 两端的出身：16 世纪教堂（禁女性歌唱 → treble 需求）与 500 年童声唱诗班传统（维也纳童声合唱团 · King's College）
八音注八音之外——民歌里的童声在牛背上（牧童），教堂里的童声在祭坛前（treble）：同一声部的两种命运

⭐⭐ 一条生长曲线，三种命运

声带起点 → 终点变化
男声17.35mm → 28.92mm+63%
女声同起点+34%
阉伶停在 14mm（尸检测值）0%

⭐⭐ 1909 年 Tandler & Gross 的尸检：「the larynx was extremely small and the thyroid notch hardly even visible……vocal chords approximately the size of a coloratura soprano! They were a mere 14mm in length」——⭐⭐ 童尺寸的声带 + 成人的肺：「the power of a man with the range of a boy」。

⭐⭐ 破音（cracking）不是失误——「serous fluid accumulates…… this excessive swelling is called oedema」：生长超过控制的生理时差。 变声期之后，声部重新洗牌——voice-types 条的「Fach 会流动」的童年版。

⭐⭐ 阉伶：为一个声音造的制度

⭐⭐ 起因：「the practice began in the 16th century, mainly for church music when women were banned from singing in sacred places」。 ⭐ 手术：「often carried out between the ages of 8 and 10……opium, ice or milk baths, barber surgeons——before anaesthetic and infection control——many didn't survive」。

⭐⭐ 制度最刺的一句自白：「sometimes the abusers did not understand that castrating a child did not make him a good singer, it just stopped his voice from breaking」——⭐⭐ 制度以为自己在制造才华，其实只是在阻止失去。

⭐⭐ 两条时间线夹住一代人：「Pope Benedict XIV attempted to ban the practice as early as 1748, but……he eventually relented, fearing it would cause church attendance to drop」；「banned by law in 1870 (Italy)」—— 一条「禁了但没禁成」的制度，和一条「禁成了但太晚了」的法律。

⭐⭐ Moreschi：一种已灭绝声音的最后影像

「Alessandro Moreschi (1858–1922), the 『last castrato』, produced in 1902 the only solo castrato recording in existence」；「recorded in the Sistine Chapel by the Gaisberg brothers, April 1902—— 17 discs in all」；「the next Pope……forbade Moreschi from making any more solo recordings when the recording machine was again brought to the Vatican in 1904」.

⭐⭐ 15 岁任圣乔瓦尼教堂第一女高音，此后西斯廷礼拜堂第一女高音 30 年； 55 岁时「small or medium stature, beardless, with a speaking voice having a metallic quality, like a very high-speaking tenor」——⭐⭐ 史上唯一有独唱录音的阉伶：一种已灭绝的人类声音的最后影像——且只是「a pale impression of what these singers were capable of」。

⭐ Farinelli 传说：「reportedly sang Philip V of Spain back to health—— a voice as sweet as a flute……like a lark intoxicated with its own flight」—— 传说与录音的差距，就是这个制度被文明化后的距离。

⭐ 童声的两种命运

传统童声在哪制度形式
教堂祭坛前的 treble切割身体（阉伶时代）
民歌牛背上的牧童自然使用（一领众和）
现代合唱团音乐厅的 treble只租借窗口期的几年

⭐⭐ 维也纳童声合唱团 · King's College Cambridge 圣诞颂歌 · 巴黎木十字—— treble 做成了五百年的机构：制度换了一种温柔的形式，不再切割身体，只租借孩子嗓子没变的几年。

⭐⭐ 而 countertenor（voice-types 条）是这次换件的现代出口：不再切身体，只练假声——同一个声部，三种时代的答案。

事实来源

  • ⭐⭐ 主线：声音的窗口期（本页最该记住的一条） —— 童声是每个人必经、又必然失去的唯一声部：变声期男子声带增长 63%（女子约 34%），「During puberty, the male vocal chords increase by 63%, while the female vocal chords increase only by about half that rate at 34%. Prepubescent male vocal chords grow from an average of 17.35mm to 28.92mm by the time they reach adulthood」；「During this change, serous fluid accumulates in the tissue. This excessive swelling is called oedema, responsible for the permanent thickening that lowers the pitch further」；⭐⭐ 全站唯一一件「会自己变质的乐器」——而人类为这个必然失去的声音，造出了音乐史上最黑暗的制度（阉伶）与最温柔的传统（童声合唱团）——一端是手术刀，一端是唱诗班
  • ⭐⭐ 阉伶：为一个声音造的制度 —— 「the practice began in the 16th century, mainly for church music when women were banned from singing in sacred places」；「the result was the power of a man with the range of a boy」；「the removal of the testes prevents the sexual dimorphism of the larynx at the time when a boy would go through puberty」；⭐⭐ 1909 年 Tandler & Gross 对一名 28 岁阉伶的尸检：「the larynx was extremely small and the thyroid notch hardly even visible……vocal chords approximately the size of a coloratura soprano! They were a mere 14mm in length」——童尺寸的声带 + 成人的肺；⭐ 手术黑暗面：「castration, often carried out between the ages of 8 and 10, was performed under grim conditions——opium, ice or milk baths, barber surgeons, before anaesthetic and infection control——many didn't survive」；⭐⭐ 最刺的一句：「sometimes the abusers did not understand that castrating a child did not make him a good singer, it just stopped his voice from breaking」——制度以为自己在制造才华，其实只是在阻止失去
  • ⭐⭐ Moreschi：唯一有录音的阉伶 —— 「Alessandro Moreschi (1858–1922), the 『last castrato』, produced in 1902 the only solo castrato recording in existence」；「probably 『altered』 around 1865, in keeping with the old practice of castrating vocally-talented boys well before puberty」；「when 15, appointed First Soprano in the choir of the Papal Basilica of San Giovanni Laterano……thereafter First Soprano of the Sistine Chapel for the next 30 years」；「recorded in the Sistine Chapel by the Gaisberg brothers, April 1902——17 discs in all」；「the next Pope took a dim view of Castrato singers and forbade Moreschi from making any more solo recordings when the recording machine was again brought to the Vatican in 1904」；「when 55, described as of small or medium stature, beardless, with a speaking voice having a metallic quality, like a very high-speaking tenor」；⭐⭐ 史上唯一有独唱录音的阉伶——「一种已灭绝的人类声音」的最后影像（且是 poor recording + past his prime——「a pale impression of what these singers were capable of」）
  • ⭐⭐ 制度的两条时间线 —— 「Pope Benedict XIV attempted to ban the practice as early as 1748, but it was so entrenched, and so popular with audiences, that he eventually relented, fearing it would cause church attendance to drop」；「castration of young males was banned by law in 1870 (Italy)——so no young castrato was being accepted into the choir」；⭐⭐ 一条「禁了但没禁成」的制度，和一条「禁成了但太晚了」的法律——中间夹着 Moreschi 的一代人（1870 年法律生效时，唱诗班里的阉伶成了最后的活化石）
  • ⭐⭐ 童声的两种命运 —— 教堂传统（treble 声部 · 女声被禁后的替代 · 「belief in innocence and purity attached to the voice」）vs 民歌传统（牧童 · 劳动 · 一领众和里的童声部）；⭐⭐ 现代童声合唱团（维也纳童声合唱团 · King's College Cambridge 的圣诞颂歌 · 巴黎木十字）把 treble 做成了五百年的机构——制度换了一种温柔的形式：不再切割身体，只租借窗口期的几年；⭐ Farinelli 传说：「reportedly sang Philip V of Spain back to health——a voice as sweet as a flute……like a lark intoxicated with its own flight」——传说与录音的差距，就是这个制度被文明化后的距离
  • ⭐⭐ 变声期之后：voice-types 条的流动性最剧烈时刻 —— 声部在一年多里重新洗牌（「a singer's Fach can evolve」的童年版本）；⭐⭐ 破音（cracking）不是失误，是生长超过控制的生理时差——全站所有乐器里，只有人声会「自己换一件」；而 countertenor（voice-types 条）是这次换件的现代出口：不再切身体，只练假声
