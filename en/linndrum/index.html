The LinnDrum

When machines began to sound like records — real drum samples, swing, and a musician who taught himself to code

⭐⭐ The main line: machines sounded like records

「Before the LinnDrum, machines sounded like machines. After the LinnDrum, they sounded like records.」

⭐⭐ Against the 808 page — the two sides of one debate: the 808's position: 「I didn't imitate the drum — I reinvented it」（the character of the artificial）; the LinnDrum's position: 「I have the one thing all previous drum machines lacked — the sound of real drums」（the truth of the recorded）.

⭐⭐ Both roads won, winning different genres: the 808 won hip-hop and techno's underground; the LinnDrum won the eighties' pop charts — 《算来算去都是假》: the analog side said 「our fake is character」; the sampling side said 「your fake is just fake」 — the real-versus-fake war defined the whole decade.

⭐⭐ Roger Linn: the musician who taught himself to code

「Linn wasn't an engineering wunderkind with a Ph.D. He was a musician. He worked with other musicians, talked to other musicians, and learned how to code and build his instruments from a place of musical necessity」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's invention genealogy: Hammond（clockmaker）· Cahill（lawyer）· Moog（engineer × composer）· Smith（the engineer who quit）— ⭐⭐ Linn is the first 「songwriting musician who taught himself to code」.

⭐⭐ 「Linn 把他的设备视为激发歌曲写作的工具，不是人类鼓手的完全替身」— the inventor's positioning decided the product's fate: positioned as a tool, it never tried to replace players — and became one.

⭐ The funding story: 「向父亲借 $20,000 资助 LM-1 开发，用自己 12 年的保时捷付 PCB 设计师」— 《行过山路记艰难》: the hardship of the mountain road bought a real-drum machine.

⭐⭐ Quantization and swing: an invention born of frustration

「Unlike the Roland drum machines that use step-sequencing, the LM-1 was programmed through live playing」; ⭐⭐ 「Roger Linn 说过他最初沮丧于机器把他的演奏量化成 16 分音符——计算机分辨率的功能。于是他引入 shuffle 功能，给交替的 16 分音符加一点延迟来注入感觉」; ⭐⭐ 「timing correction 与 swing 的组合是决定性的配方——定义了此后所有的鼓机」.

⭐⭐ Against the 909's shuffle knob — the patent for 「human timing as a control」 was first filed under Linn's name. ⭐⭐ The path of invention: quantization was a flaw（not enough resolution）; swing was the remedy（adding humanity back）— two 「mistakes」 stacked into an industry standard.

⭐⭐ Leon Russell's two features（user co-creation）

「Leon Russell——在 1979 年专辑 Life and Love 上用早期原型—— 贡献了两个成为行业标准的功能：handclap 采样与 swing timing 函数」.

⭐⭐ Against Moog × Deutsch（the moog-modular page） — user co-creation again: and this co-creation's two features became industry standards. 《真心阿妹唔论家穷》: true-hearted drums ask no wealth of the machine — true-hearted co-creation asks no rank of the user.

⭐⭐ The LinnDrum（LM-2 · 1982）: democratised at half price

「1982 年发布的 LinnDrum（LM-2）巩固了那个十年的采样声音—— 更舒服的形态，价格几乎减半（$2,995）」; ⭐⭐ 「15 种声音存在 socketed EPROM 芯片里——让录音室可以换入换出声音」; ⭐⭐ 「8-bit mu-law 编码——从 8-bit 存储给出 12–13 bit 的等效动态范围」; 「15 个独立输出——每鼓一轨——在鼓机还是单输出的年代，这是革命性的」.

⭐⭐ Against the Juno（democratisation）and the ARP（education market）— the LinnDrum's democratisation ran on 「half price」 and 「swappable chips」: sounds became replaceable parts — the sample library was born here.

⭐⭐ The player lineage: the skeleton of eighties pop

「Prince 把它用在 1999（1982）到 Purple Rain（1984）几乎每张唱片」; ⭐⭐ 「Thriller 专辑（1982）用了 hybrid 设置：LM-2 机身 + LM-1 音色芯片的 snare 与 congas + 一颗 TR-808 的 clap 芯片」 — rival chips inside one machine; 「Kate Bush 的 Running Up That Hill（1985）围绕一个 LinnDrum pattern 构建」; 「a-ha 的 Take On Me——制作人 Alan Tarney 说他从未见过任何人把 Linn 编得这么好」.

⭐⭐ Thriller's mixed chips are the site's cleverest stroke: the LM-2's body + the LM-1's snare + the 808's clap — rivals' chips plugged into one machine, carrying the best-selling album ever. ⭐ Against the polysynth page's E-mu thread: gear has factions; music does not.

⭐⭐ The Linn 9000 and the company's death

「Linn Electronics 1986 年 2 月关闭」; 「原定的后继 Linn 9000 遭遇严重的可靠性问题，耗尽了公司资源」; 「MIDI 已成为竞争机器的标准，而 LinnDrum 一台都没有」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's company histories: Smith sold · Moog sold · Korg bought back · Fairlight folded — Linn's death is the most poignant: the double death of missing MIDI and a failed product. （The man who invented 「the formula that defined every drum machine after」 lost to the interface he never fitted.）

⭐⭐ Roger Linn's second act: the MPC60（1988）

「Linn Electronics 关闭后，Roger Linn 加入 Akai 并设计了 MPC60—— 引入 velocity-sensitive pads 与定义此后三十年 beat production 工作流的乐器」; ⭐⭐ 「每一台 Akai MPC 都直接溯源于 Linn 的架构」; 「SP-1200 与 MPC60 在 80 年代末 90 年代初瓜分了 hip-hop 制作」.

⭐⭐ Against the site: the inventor's second act — Fairlight's Vogel turned to film · Sequential's Smith returned（MIDI/DSI）· Linn joined Akai — companies die; inventors walk into the next machine.

⭐⭐ 「2012 年 Linn 与 Dave Smith 合作 Tempest」 — the only instrument co-signed by the two giants（father of sampled drums × father of programmable polyphony）.

⭐⭐ Behringer LM Drum（2024）: the clone debate with a living inventor

「Behringer LM Drum（2024）是低价克隆——不过 Roger Linn 公开表示他未被咨询，更希望 Behringer 在复制设计前先征得他的许可」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's reissue genealogy: the Minimoog（official reissue）· the TB-303（countless clones, designer gone）· the Nord Modular（official continuation）— ⭐⭐ the Linn case asked 「the ethics of cloning」 to a living inventor for the first time. No consensus yet — but the question has entered history.
