The sequencer

Time turned into knobs — a clock pushing a row of potentiometers, performance becoming setting

Related

Voltage controlMoog modular synthesizerProphet-5Hammond organPitchArticulation Marks

Every synthesizer in the previous pages needed a 「hand」 — keys pressed, knobs turned, drums struck.

This page's instrument dispenses with the hand: a row of knobs and a clock, and the music turns by itself.

「时钟一个一个地步进这些电位器，然后循环回开头。」

⭐⭐ This page is the sequencer — ninth in the analog-synth family. One line: time turned into knobs.

⭐⭐ The main line: time turned into knobs

「一排电位器与一只『时钟』（脉冲发生器）相连：时钟一个一个地步进这些电位器，然后循环回开头；音序器的输出作为控制电压与门脉冲喂给合成器。通过『调音』这些电位器，一段短小的重复节奏动机就被设定好了」.

⭐⭐ Performance became setting; time became knobs — music could 「turn」 with no one playing it.

⭐⭐ The folk mirrors: 《驴车转》 — a clock pushing the knobs round, like a donkey at a mill; what comes out is not flour but melody. 《轮到我唱歌歌不来》 — wherever the clock steps, that knob's value is the current note; a knob twisted to silence is the 「song not coming」.

⭐⭐ The pre-history: Raymond Scott's electromechanical giants

「Raymond Scott 在 1940–50 年代设计并建造了一些最早的电机械音乐音序器—— 『Wall of Sound』：一个房间大小的继电器系统」; 「1959 年前后的 Circle Machine：旋转的光电管与可调的灯」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's invention genealogy: clockmaker Hammond（frequency cast in gears）· lawyer Cahill（200 tons of generators）— ⭐⭐ Scott was the first 「composer who built his own machine」 （what he wanted was music without performers）.

⭐⭐ The RCA punch tape: an expensive lesson

「RCA Mark II……通过打孔纸带间接控制——用户通过在纸上打孔编程作品，一个痛苦的过程，需要预先规划每一个音符。没有实时演奏，没有即兴。 Milton Babbitt 花数月编程持续几分钟的作品」.

⭐⭐ Against the RCA of the moog-modular page（「programmable, not playable」）— the punch tape proved that 「programmable」 was not enough; it had to be 「immediate」.

⭐⭐ Buchla 123/146: the revolution of the looping pattern

「Don Buchla 于 1965 年完成 Buchla 100 系列，内含世界第一台商用步进音序器—— 8 步与 16 步模块（123 与 146）」; 「Buchla 的音序器输出 CV，可被 patch 到任何目的地：音高、滤波截止、振幅」.

⭐⭐ 「这是革命性的。不再提前编程完整作品，音乐家可以设定 通过模块化接线而演化的循环图样。」

⭐⭐ Against the tape-music page（concrète = pre-edited） — Buchla's looping pattern was improvisation's twin sibling: tape turned past performances into material; the sequencer turned future performances into settings.

⭐⭐ The Moog 960: a paradigm for fifty years

「三年后，Robert Moog 推出 960 Sequential Controller—— 大量基于 Buchla 的设计但加了内置时钟； 三排平行 × 每排八步，产生三路同时的 CV 序列；通过 962 Sequential Switch 链接多台，最多 24 步」.

⭐⭐ 「960 确立了步进音序器范式： 一排排旋钮每步一只 · 一只时钟步进 · 可调长度的循环 · 输出 CV 到合成器参数—— 这个格式影响了此后五十年的硬件音序器。」

⭐⭐ 「Limitation becomes aesthetics」: the Berlin School

「Tangerine Dream、Klaus Schulze、Kraftwerk 围绕序列化的图样构建了整张专辑; Phaedra（1974）与 Rubycon（1975）里，催眠而演化的序列跑满整面唱片」.

⭐⭐ The most important summary on this page:

「限制成了美学。八或十六步，无限循环，用滤波与音高的微妙变化制造运动—— 『柏林学派』的电子音乐诞生于步进音序器的限制。」

⭐⭐ Against the site's 「limits become virtues」: the niutuiqin's narrow range hugging the voice · the xun's muddy low matching Song aesthetics — the Berlin School is the same event replayed on an electronic instrument.

⭐⭐ Phaedra's drift: the fourth 「imprecise = alive」

「在 Phaedra 著名的开场里，音序器已经走音—— 可以清楚听到 Chris Franke 实时用耳朵重新调音」.

⭐⭐ The fourth documented case: the Hammond key click · electric-guitar feedback · the Minimoog's drift · the Moog 960's out-of-tune Phaedra opening — and the liveliest yet: not a flaw admired after the fact, but a performance saved in real time by an attentive ear. A drifting sequencer + an agile ear = an opening nobody could replicate.

⭐⭐ Kraftwerk: the sequencer on the motorway

「Kraftwerk 在 1974 年专辑 Autobahn 上推进了音序器的应用—— 用模拟音序器生成标志性的 motorik beats——120–130 BPM 的重复 4/4 图样，模仿高速公路的持续脉动，定义了 krautrock 的电子美学」.

⭐⭐ motorik（「motoric」）— for the first time the sequencer's endless loop was not a defect, but the 「motorway」 itself.

⭐⭐ Three live techniques

① Real-time switches: 「为避免无限重复的单调，Chris Franke 与 Michael Hoenig 会在演奏中实时操作这些开关——从序列中加入与丢掉音符和节拍」（each step's switch has three states: play · skip · loop back）.

② One pitch row, many oscillators: 「音高排 patch 到两只以上按音程调音的振荡器，振荡器一次一个地混进混出」.

③ Twin sequencers phasing: 「同步两台音序器并分别操作——立起 Steve Reich 式的旋转复节奏相位图样」.

⭐ Klaus Schulze's transposition trick: 「音序器的输出电压加到键盘控制器的输出电压上—— 后者用来现场移调整条序列」（Bayreuth Return · Floating）.

⭐⭐ The ARP 1050: sequencing audio, not just voltage

「ARP 2500 常配 1050 Mix-Sequencer——不同于只产生电压的当代音序器， 1050 也能序列化音频信号——序列的每一步可以来自完全不同的声源」; 「它那垂直一列 8 个白色方形按钮会亮起指示活动步」.

⭐⭐ Against the Moog 960（sequencing voltage）— the 1050 sequenced 「sound itself」: eight steps, eight timbres — a sampling band inside one machine.

⭐⭐ The TR-808: step programming as drum mother-tongue

「步进编程：用户在 16 步网格上一次一步输入图样，点按按钮放置声音 · 图样链接：最多 32 个图样链成歌 · accent track：一条独立的力度音序轨道 · 独立输出」 — 「808 的步进输入工作流成了鼓编程的模板」（see the drum-machine entries）.

Recap: three sentences on the sequencer

Before the sequencer, repetition lived in a player's muscles; after it, repetition lived in a clock.

eight steps turning ten thousand times, with the filter changing every lap, is scenery.
