Children's voices and the voice break

The window — the voice part everyone passes through and everyone loses

Related

VoiceVoice classificationPitchTimbre

⭐⭐ The main line: the window

「During puberty, the male vocal chords increase by 63%, while the female vocal chords increase only by about half that rate at 34%. Prepubescent male vocal chords grow from an average of 17.35mm to 28.92mm by the time they reach adulthood」.

⭐⭐ The child's voice is the one voice part everyone passes through and everyone loses — the only instrument on this site that 「spoils on its own」. ⭐⭐ And for this necessarily lost sound, humanity built both the darkest institution in musical history (the castrati) and the gentlest tradition (the boys' choirs) — one end a scalpel, the other a choir stall.

⭐⭐ The 1909 Tandler & Gross autopsy: 「the larynx was extremely small and the thyroid notch hardly even visible……vocal chords approximately the size of a coloratura soprano! They were a mere 14mm in length」 — ⭐⭐ child-size folds plus an adult man's lungs: 「the power of a man with the range of a boy」.

⭐⭐ The crack is not a mistake — 「serous fluid accumulates……this excessive swelling is called oedema」: a physiological lag of control behind growth. After the break, the voice part is reshuffled — the childhood version of the voice-types entry's 「Fach can evolve」.

⭐⭐ The castrato: an institution built for one sound

⭐⭐ The cause: 「the practice began in the 16th century, mainly for church music when women were banned from singing in sacred places」. ⭐ The surgery: 「often carried out between the ages of 8 and 10……opium, ice or milk baths, barber surgeons — before anaesthetic and infection control — many didn't survive」.

⭐⭐ The system's most piercing self-confession: 「sometimes the abusers did not understand that castrating a child did not make him a good singer, it just stopped his voice from breaking」 — ⭐⭐ the institution thought it was manufacturing talent; it was only preventing loss.

⭐⭐ Two timelines holding a generation: 「Pope Benedict XIV attempted to ban the practice as early as 1748, but……he eventually relented, fearing it would cause church attendance to drop」; 「banned by law in 1870 (Italy)」 — a ban that failed, and a law that succeeded too late.

⭐⭐ Moreschi: the last image of an extinct voice

「Alessandro Moreschi (1858–1922), the 『last castrato』, produced in 1902 the only solo castrato recording in existence」; 「recorded in the Sistine Chapel by the Gaisberg brothers, April 1902 — 17 discs in all」; 「the next Pope……forbade Moreschi from making any more solo recordings when the recording machine was again brought to the Vatican in 1904」.

⭐⭐ First soprano of San Giovanni in Laterano at fifteen, then First Soprano of the Sistine Chapel for thirty years; at fifty-five 「of small or medium stature, beardless, with a speaking voice having a metallic quality, like a very high-speaking tenor」 — ⭐⭐ the only castrato with a solo recording: the last image of an extinct human voice — and only 「a pale impression of what these singers were capable of」.

⭐ The Farinelli legend: 「reportedly sang Philip V of Spain back to health — a voice as sweet as a flute……like a lark intoxicated with its own flight」 — the distance between legend and recording is the distance this institution travelled to be civilised.

⭐ Two fates of the child's voice

⭐⭐ Vienna Boys' Choir · King's College Cambridge's Christmas carols · Les Petits Chanteurs à la Croix de Bois — the treble became a 500-year institution: the system took a gentler form, no longer cutting bodies, only renting the years before a child's voice changes.

⭐⭐ And the countertenor (voice-types entry) is the modern outlet for this swap: no longer cutting the body, only training falsetto — one voice part, three eras' answers.

  • ⭐⭐ 制度的两条时间线 —— 「Pope Benedict XIV attempted to ban the practice as early as 1748, but it was so entrenched, and so popular with audiences, that he eventually relented, fearing it would cause church attendance to drop」；「castration of young males was banned by law in 1870 (Italy)——so no young castrato was being accepted into the choir」；⭐⭐ 一条「禁了但没禁成」的制度，和一条「禁成了但太晚了」的法律——中间夹着 Moreschi 的一代人（1870 年法律生效时，唱诗班里的阉伶成了最后的活化石）
  • ⭐⭐ 童声的两种命运 —— 教堂传统（treble 声部 · 女声被禁后的替代 · 「belief in innocence and purity attached to the voice」）vs 民歌传统（牧童 · 劳动 · 一领众和里的童声部）；⭐⭐ 现代童声合唱团（维也纳童声合唱团 · King's College Cambridge 的圣诞颂歌 · 巴黎木十字）把 treble 做成了五百年的机构——制度换了一种温柔的形式：不再切割身体，只租借窗口期的几年；⭐ Farinelli 传说：「reportedly sang Philip V of Spain back to health——a voice as sweet as a flute……like a lark intoxicated with its own flight」——传说与录音的差距，就是这个制度被文明化后的距离
