The software synthesizer

The synthesizer became code — the CPU the only limit

Related

The DX7 and FM synthesisTB-303The MIDI controllerModular and EurorackTimbrePitch

Dozens of instruments with 「bodies」 have been written in this section — wood, metal, chips, cases.

This page's instrument has no body: it is a piece of code, living inside your computer.

「房间里的合成器现在可以嵌进任何电子设备的单芯片，或被软件精确仿真」.

⭐⭐ This page is the software synthesizer — eighth and last of the digital-synth family. One line: the synthesizer became code.

⭐⭐ The main line: the synthesizer became code

「一旦 GigaSampler 与 Bitheadz Unity DS-1 证明电脑也能处理采样—— 电脑成为终极音源再无障碍——CPU 算力是唯一的限制」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's mass curve: the 200-ton Telharmonium → the Moog wall → the 14-kg Minimoog → the 2.5-kg TB-303 → ⭐⭐ the zero-hardware software synthesizer — the instrument's mass went to zero and the sound did not. 《算盘子》: what the abacus counts out is not debt — it is waveforms.

⭐⭐ ReBirth: the classic ahead of the standard

「1997 年 Propellerheads 发布的程序革命了电脑音乐业—— ReBirth RB-338 是一对 Roland TB-303 贝斯合成器与一台 TR-808 节奏机的漂亮仿真」; 「如果 VST 乐器标准 1999 年才有，ReBirth（1996 发布）其实领先于游戏」.

⭐⭐ 「忠于原机界面的仿真是它成功的关键——当时电脑与声卡技术还不足以从软件实时生成声音」— the step sequencer + 16 pads bypassed the keyboard problem: anyone could start making grooves immediately, without a keyboard, and without being able to play one.

⭐⭐ 「ReBirth 是 smash hit，其制作者两年后发布了完整 DAW——Reason」— a 「simulation program」 raised a DAW company.

⭐⭐ The birth of VSTi: ReWire and ASIO

「Steinberg 发布了 Virtual Studio Technology（VST）架构——为录音软件带来真正的『live』混音与实时效果插件」; 「Propellerhead 想把 ReBirth 接进 VST——他们的方案是 ReWire： ReBirth 输出与 Cubase VST 混音器之间的桥」.

⭐⭐ 「这一发展启发 Steinberg 创建 VST 引擎的扩展——允许软件合成器『插件』被使用—— 称为 VST Instruments（VSTi）」.

⭐⭐ But the VSTi's success hinged on an 「elusive」 ability — playing in real time: 「Steinberg 的 ASIO 驱动是钥匙——ASIO 2.0 与 VST 2.0（1999）首次把延迟降到 10ms 以下， VSTi 才真正可弹.」 Against the site: 「10ms」 — the gap between the key and the sound that no ear can hear — the software synthesizer waited two years for exactly those ten milliseconds.

⭐⭐ NI Pro-Five: the first hardware-emulation plugin

「VST2 的黎明，Native Instruments 发布 Pro-Five——他们对 1970 年代 Prophet-5 的插件仿真」; 「从今天的立场看很怪，但真实硬件合成器的插件仿真是当时全新的东西」.

⭐⭐ Against the TB-303's clone chain: the 303's sound was first hardware-cloned（Behringer TD-3）; the Prophet's was first software-emulated（Pro-Five）— cloning and emulation started running at the same time, both toward 「resurrecting lost instruments」.

⭐⭐ Nord Lead and Virtual Analog: red case, full of knobs

「Swedish 公司 Clavia 1995 年发布 Nord Lead——点燃了一个认识：软件可以仿真简单电路的振荡器与滤波器，仿真到感觉像硬件」; 「创始人 Hans Nordelius 发现 DSP（原本跑音频效果的）也能模拟老模拟合成器里电路的行为—— 他称之为 Virtual Analog」.

⭐⭐ 「Nord Lead，醒目的红色机身，罕见地满是旋钮，引起轰动」— against the Minimoog's 「knobs lead」: the Nord Lead restored the dignity of knobs in the digital era — the foreshadowing of the analog revival ten years later.

⭐⭐ Nord Modular: modular inside the screen

「Nord Modular……他们有个看似疯狂的想法：你可以在 PC 上编辑整个合成引擎」; 「在屏幕上编辑合成——自 Fairlight CMI 这类乐器以来未曾见过」.

⭐⭐ 「在此之前，模块化合成是老旧、巨大、昂贵的退化电子设备机架， patched together with swathes of cables，没有保存 patch 的概念—— 很少有人能接触任何模块化」; 「Nord Modular 以非常合理的价格提供完全模块化的探险乐园—— 在电脑上用鼠标，构建自己的合成器、保存它、再构建完全不同的另一个」.

⭐⭐ Against the eurorack page: software put 「modules + patch cables」 inside the screen — even the patch cable became virtual.

⭐⭐ 「We were really stupid in the 1990s」

「我们 90 年代的人真的蠢，至少主流合成器技术圈的人是—— 我们把真实与模仿看得高于一切，把 shaping waveforms 与 individual controls 当作 tired and unfashionable 而搁置」; 「现代 PCM 合成器带来极大的便利与巨大的音色范围，却在可用性与性格上失去了东西」; 「home-spun 的 rave 与 dance 音乐 genres 太清楚这一点，急切地抢购所有能找到的二手模拟合成器」.

⭐⭐ Against Schaeffer's late confession（「Do-Re-Mi 之外没有路」）— two generations of self-examination thirty years apart: one admitted the boundary of exploration, one the price of arrogance.

⭐⭐ Software made 「the perfect digital」 and in doing so made 「the imperfect analog」 costlier — the analog revival（from the late 90s; affordable new machines by Moog/Korg/Arturia/DSI in the 2010s）was a counter-force the software world triggered itself.

This section has written the whole journey from 200 tons to zero hardware: the Telharmonium's weight, the Moog wall, the Minimoog's wood, the TB-303's plastic — finally the instrument became 「nothing」, and the sound remained sound.

it lives inside everything that can count.
