The TR-808

Drums sung by out-of-spec transistors — a commercial flop became rhythm's common tongue

2 min readElectronicUpdated 2026-10-05

instrumentelectronicelectrophonewesterndrum-machinetr-808

It set out to copy drums and invented them instead

In 1980 Roland put a black box on the shelf with Rhythm Composer printed across the front. Press one of sixteen coloured buttons, and kick, snare and hi-hat line up; there is no drumhead and no sample. It was meant to imitate real drums, but memory for samples cost too much, so the engineers built the sounds piece by piece in analog circuits.

The secret weapon was a batch of rejected transistors

The 808's sizzle comes from the 2SC828-R. The batch had been rejected as out of specification, and Roland bought it cheap for the voice circuits. The parts were not faulty; they simply behaved oddly, and the hi-hat and cymbals picked up that grainy edge. When the batch ran out, the machine was discontinued. Reissues cannot reproduce the sound, and the reason is not on the schematic. A circuit can be copied; a rejected production run cannot.

Sixteen buttons fixed a grammar

TR-REC writes rhythm with a row of sixteen buttons, one step per button, and a light crossing the row is one bar. It was not the first sequencer, but it was the first notation that a large number of musicians learned: sixteen steps, one hit per step, repeating. Forty years later nearly every drum machine and plug-in still uses that grid. The TB-303, released a year earlier by the same company, ran on the same step logic, and bass and drums have shared a unit of time ever since.

A commercial flop, then a common tongue

The 808 was made for two years, about twelve thousand units. In 1982 the LinnDrum arrived, sampling became the new direction, and the 808 looked obsolete. It was discounted into second-hand shops and picked up by young musicians in Chicago, Detroit and Manchester. Stretch the kick's decay and the drum turns into a bass note; push the low end hard enough and speaker cones blow, which made speaker makers happy. Marvin Gaye used it on Sexual Healing, Afrika Bambaataa on Planet Rock, and after that its shadow falls across hip-hop, house, techno and trap. It did not imitate the drum; it rebuilt it. Every 11 August is 808 Day, and a failed machine has a holiday of its own.

  • 主线：废件晶体管唱出的鼓（本页最该记住的一条） —— 「被拒绝为『out of specification』的晶体管被 Roland 买下，用作 TR-808 发声系统的一部分；它们并非有缺陷，而是展现出非常特别的品质——给了 808 独特的 sizzling（嘶嘶）声音特色」； 「一颗小小的 2SC828-R 对最终音色如此重要，以至供应耗尽时 TR-808 停产」；「这也是大部分现代模拟鼓机无法真正复刻 TR-808 独特音色的部分原因」→ 其他条目「缺点变签名」的硬件版巅峰：缺陷不在设计里，在元器件的「不合格」里——连复刻都复刻不了
  • 模拟 vs 采样的抉择（出于需要的选择） —— 「目标搭载真鼓的音色。但当时能够回放真实鼓声采样的存储芯片造价过于昂贵，因此工程师们选择用模拟合成的方式再现真鼓的声音——先用 Roland 的 System-700 模块化系统创建鼓音色，再在 TR-808 的模拟电路上用参数重建出来」； 「虽然是出于需要的选择，但采用模拟技术而非数字技术将对未来数年的音乐产生深远的影响」——对照 TB-303（同一家公司、同一种「省钱的选择」）：两次「被迫模拟」，两次改写音乐史
  • TR-REC：音序法的诞生（沿用至今） —— 「前面板印着『Rhythm Composer』（节奏创作者）——设计者们的初衷是创作节奏」；「通过一排 16 个不同颜色的按钮，用户可以编程并存储自己的节奏」； 「这也是 Roland 首次使用 TR-REC 的方法来创作鼓的节奏，并沿用至今」（开发者：菊本忠男团队的中村浩 Hiro Nakamura 开发模拟复音电路）——对照 TB-303 的 16 步音序器：同一家人，同一种步进哲学
  • Speaker Killer（扬声器杀手） —— 「由于 808 是合成的声音，调节音色成为可能：调 hi-hat 的嘶嘶、snare 的脆、最关键的是 kick 更有 punch」；「把底鼓的衰减拉长会揭示更多温暖的模拟音色——使得 808 的音乐有让人们舞动起来的力量」； 「一打开的 808 曲目的极低频会定期吹破喇叭盆——音箱厂商们因为能卖出更多产品而十分开心」——一台乐器以「弄坏别的设备」闻名，史无前例
  • 生不逢时→二手传奇（与 TB-303 完全同构） —— 「TR-808 实际上只生产两年（1980–1982），约 12,000 台——商业失败」；「1982 年竞品 LinnDrum 发布，帮助把趋势从模拟转向数字采样鼓机——808 看起来时代已过」； 「停产后的老故事重演：二手店里的机器被爱冒险与破规矩的年轻艺术家迅速抢购」；「从芝加哥到底特律，从曼彻斯特到迈阿密」—— TB-303 与 808 是同一家公司（Roland）的「双子失败」：都是约一万台、都被二手少年捡走、都命名了一个流派
