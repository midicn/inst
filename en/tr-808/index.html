The TR-808

Drums sung by out-of-spec transistors — a commercial flop became rhythm's common tongue

Related

TB-303SequencerMinimoogElectric guitarArticulation MarksPitch

The previous page turned the instrument into code — the next few pages look back at the 「wrong machines」 that invented rhythm's common tongue.

「The 808 didn't imitate the drums — it reinvented them.」

⭐⭐ This page is the TR-808 — first of the drum-machine family. One line: drums sung by out-of-spec transistors.

ClassAssignment
HS classElectrophone（analog subtractive drum synthesis + 16-step sequencer）· 12 simultaneous sounds
Origin⭐⭐ 1980, Roland（the Kikumoto team; Hiro Nakamura's analog voice circuits）— 「Rhythm Composer」 is its real name
Two numbers⭐⭐ about 12,000 units（discontinued two years later） · used on more hit records than any other drum machine
Bayin noteOutside the bayin — the drum that needed no drumhead

⭐⭐ The main line: drums sung by out-of-spec transistors

「被拒绝为『out of specification』的晶体管被 Roland 买下，用作 TR-808 发声系统的一部分；它们并非有缺陷，而是展现出非常特别的品质——给了 808 独特的 sizzling（嘶嘶）声音特色」.

⭐⭐ 「一颗小小的 2SC828-R 对最终音色如此重要，以至供应耗尽时 TR-808 停产」—

⭐⭐ 「这也是大部分现代模拟鼓机无法真正复刻 TR-808 音色的部分原因」.

⭐⭐ The hardware peak of the site's 「flaw into signature」: the flaw is not in the design but in the 「out-of-spec」 components — even the reissue cannot replicate it, because out-of-spec transistors are no longer made. 《打铁硪号》: the smith knows — the off-spec material is often the good one.

⭐⭐ Analog versus sampling: a choice forced by necessity

「目标搭载真鼓的音色。但当时能够回放真实鼓声采样的存储芯片造价过于昂贵—— 因此工程师们选择用模拟合成的方式再现真鼓的声音：先用 Roland 的 System-700 模块化系统创建鼓音色，再在 TR-808 的模拟电路上用参数重建出来」.

⭐⭐ 「虽然是出于需要的选择，但采用模拟技术而非数字技术将对未来数年的音乐产生深远的影响」—

⭐⭐ Against the TB-303（the same Roland, the same 「money-saving choice」）: two forced-analog decisions, two rewritten music histories — the timbre squeezed out by 「expensive」 proved more immortal than the one by 「cheap」.

⭐⭐ TR-REC: the sequencing method that never left

「前面板印着『Rhythm Composer』——设计者们的初衷是创作节奏；通过一排 16 个不同颜色的按钮，用户可以编程并存储自己的节奏」; ⭐⭐ 「这也是 Roland 首次使用 TR-REC 的方法来创作鼓的节奏，并沿用至今」.

⭐⭐ Against the TB-303's 16-step sequencer — one family（Roland）, one step philosophy: 「16 steps」 became the common grammar of drums and bass. （Development: Hiro Nakamura, on the Kikumoto team, built the analog voice circuits.）

⭐⭐ Speaker Killer: famous for breaking other gear

「由于 808 是合成的声音，调节音色成为可能：调 hi-hat 的嘶嘶、snare 的脆、最关键的是 kick 更有 punch——把底鼓的衰减拉长会揭示更多温暖的模拟音色」; ⭐⭐ 「一打开的 808 曲目的极低频会定期吹破喇叭盆—— 音箱厂商们因为能卖出更多产品而十分开心」.

⭐⭐ Against the site: the Minimoog's 「one note levels the room」 was rhetoric — ⭐⭐ the 808's blown speaker cones were physics. An instrument famous for breaking other equipment: unprecedented.

⭐⭐ Wrong era → second-hand legend（exactly the TB-303 structure）

「TR-808 实际上只生产两年（1980–1982），约 12,000 台——商业失败」; 「1982 年竞品 LinnDrum 发布，帮助把趋势从模拟转向数字采样鼓机—— 808 看起来时代已过」; ⭐⭐ 「停产后的老故事重演：二手店里的机器被爱冒险与破规矩的年轻艺术家迅速抢购—— 从芝加哥到底特律，从曼彻斯特到迈阿密」.

⭐⭐ The TB-303 and the 808 are the「twin failures」of one company（Roland）: about ten thousand units each, picked up by second-hand teenagers, each naming a genre. ⭐⭐ Roland failed twice and rewrote music history twice — the failure warehouse is the world's most important instrument museum.

⭐⭐ Status: rhythm's common tongue

「808 最终被用于比任何其它鼓机更多的热门唱片」; ⭐⭐ 「它在 hip-hop 中的特别流行使其成为流行音乐中最有影响力的发明之一—— comparable to the Fender Stratocaster's impact on rock」; 「早期金曲：Marvin Gaye 的 Sexual Healing · Afrika Bambaataa 的 Planet Rock」; 「Electro · pop · soul · hip hop · techno · house · trap · Miami bass」.

⭐⭐ 「Comparable to the Stratocaster」 — against the electric-guitar page: one guitar defined rock; one drum machine defined hip-hop — the first time an instrument's cultural weight could be compared across categories.

⭐⭐ Two closing quotes

「The 808 didn't imitate the drums — it reinvented them.」

「You look at the circuit diagram like you look at an orchestral score.」

⭐⭐ The engineer's schematic and the composer's score are the same document — this page's instrument is a band 「read」 out of a circuit diagram.
