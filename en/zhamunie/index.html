Zhamunie

A name that is an opinion — "the pleasing sound"

4 min readChinese PluckedUpdated 2026-09-28

instrumentpluckedchordophoneChinesebayinsilk

Related

PitchTimbreDombraTanburRuan

Its name is the least name-like name in this batch: not a shape, not a material, not an origin — an opinion.

In Tibetan, "zha" (扎木) means sound and "nye" (聂) means pleasing to the ear — so "zhamunie" means "the instrument whose sound is pleasing." (Written སྒྲ་སྙན་, Wylie sgra snyan.)

Rule ㊾: a name that is an opinion — it does not say "what I look like" but "whether I sound good."

A cut-open gourd, a long fretless neck

Three points:

1. "Fretless" is its mark. "Its neck is longer than other instruments' and has no frets" — set beside the dutar (16–18), tanbur (16–20-plus) and dombra (8–10-plus), it stands out: same long-necked family, but it does not divide the string into frets. 2. Six strings in three pairs. "Adjacent strings form pairs, three pairs in all" — the fifth division of strings in this batch (after one-versus-many, grouping, melody-plus-drone, outer-plus-inner). 3. The parts have local names. The body is called a "pond" in Tibetan — an instrument with its own vocabulary at home.

One instrument, six styles

The dombra page said: an instrument can have dialects. The zhamunie gives a tidier proof — six style schools:

"By geography and local dance style, the zhanian formed the Ngari, Tingri, Lhatse, Lhasa, Gongbo and Amdo style schools."

One instrument, one name, six ways of playing on one plateau. (⚠️ Tibetan areas differ across Tibet, Qinghai, Sichuan, Yunnan and Gansu, which is part of why the styles multiply; only the six names the source gives are listed here, with no map drawn.)

Its posture deserves a line of its own: slung on the shoulder · seated on the ground · played while dancing — an instrument you play sitting down and also on the move: its music is not for listening to but for moving together.

Three contemporary notes, all recent:

  • 2014: "zhanian making technique" entered the fourth national ICH list — the craft itself is now protected;
  • 26 July 2026: Gonghe County, Qinghai, gathered 626 players for the "largest Tibetan zhamunie singing" event, certified as a world record;
  • ⚠️ And an old clue: "on ancient murals one sees zhamunies both plucked by hand and bowed" — one instrument, two historical techniques.

Range

Common misconceptions

  • "It came from the Central Plains." ⚠️ Three origins coexist: an Indian multi-string ancestor, a Tang-dynasty arrival from the interior (recorded in the Red Annals), and most Tibetan scholars' view that it is native; plus a myth linking it to the goddess of music. Set side by side.
  • "Six-string means six courses." ⚠️ Six strings, two to a pair, three pairs; and other versions exist (three-, four-, eight-string).
  • "Long-necked lutes all have frets." ⚠️ This one is fretless — unlike the dutar, tanbur and dombra.
  • "It only accompanies." ⚠️ It also solos (mostly dance tunes: Ama Lehua, Dawa Shungnu, *Yiji Changmu*) — and its song form is itself national heritage.
  • "The name is an empty transliteration." ⚠️ The opposite: it means "the instrument with a pleasing sound" — the only "review-style" name in this batch.

Timbre, and how to hear it

Three cues:

1. Hear "full and ringing." The sources' two words for it — a skinned box with paired strings makes it hold its own across a dance floor. 2. Hear the frets' absence. Without frets, slides are freer — it has more "inflection" than "grid." 3. Hear the floor beneath the dance. In Nangma, Toi and opera it is rarely the star: it keeps the dancing steady. Its sound is the layer under the feet.

Sources

  • 名称（一句评价）：「藏语中『扎木』意为声音，『聂』意为悦耳好听，因此『扎木聂』的意思是声音悦耳的琴」；「藏语：སྒྲ་སྙན་，威利转写 sgra snyan……藏语中意为『悦耳的琴音』」；「藏语『扎』是声音，『聂』为悦耳好听之意，『扎木聂』意为悦耳的琴声」；「『扎』意为音，『年』（或『念』）意为悦耳，合起来意为『悦耳动听之声』」；别名：「曾译称扎年 · 木聂 · 占木聂 · 扎木年」，「不同地区或文献中，还有『拜翁』『拜帛』『归布』等别名」；汉语俗称「六弦琴」依藏学与非遗资料
  • 起源三说与传说：「其起源众说不一，约略有 3 种说法：① 古代由印度传入西藏的一种多弦乐器演变而成；② 部分学者认为唐代从内地传入西藏的乐器；③ 多数西藏学者认为源于西藏本土，是藏族自己创造的乐器」；「据藏文史书《红史》载，扎木聂于唐代从内地传入」；「也有神话传说将其与『妙音天女』相联系」；「早在 1300 多年的松赞干布时期以前，就有了较完整的歌舞……从古代壁画上，可见到既有用手拔弹的札木聂，也有用弓拉奏的札木聂」依藏学与非遗资料
  • 形制：「音箱为木制，形状像一个切开的葫芦。其上部较小，呈棱形；下部较大，呈椭圆形，面皮通常是蟒皮或羊皮。其琴柱较其他乐器长且无品」；《辞海》：「琴身木制，共鸣箱似切开的葫芦状，以蛇皮或羊皮蒙面，琴杆较长，无品，张六弦，相邻两弦各为一组，分三组，按四度关系定弦」；「共鸣箱（藏族俗称『池塘』）似切开的半葫芦形，长 27 厘米左右，用整块木料制作……下部挖出椭圆形腹腔，表面蒙以山羊皮 · 獐子皮或鱼皮，面宽 14—16 厘米」；材料「多使用桑木 · 红木 · 核桃木 · 檀香木制作，以红木制品为佳」；「琴体大小不一，常见者全长 100 厘米—110 厘米」依辞书与百科资料
  • 弦与定弦：「张六条丝弦，两弦为一组，按四 · 五度定弦 a · d¹ · g」（另记「常见定弦为 E3 · A3 · D4 · G4」·「系以四度定弦」）；「有三弦琴 · 四弦琴 · 六弦琴和八弦琴等种类」（另记「六弦 · 八弦 · 十六弦 · 二十弦等，其中以六弦琴最为普遍」）—— ⚠️ 两处口径不同，并列备查依藏学与百科资料
  • 演奏与用途：「演奏时，演奏者将琴置于肩上，左手持琴按弦，右手执拨子弹奏（类似阮咸的奏法）」；「琴挎于肩上……右手执牛角拨子弹奏。也常席地坐弹或边弹边舞」；「是西藏古典歌舞囊玛以及民间歌舞堆谐（堆谢）的主要伴奏乐器，也可用于器乐合奏或独奏」；「主要流传于拉萨 · 山南 · 阿里 · 拉孜（巴谐）· 萨迦 · 昂仁 · 定日等地」；独奏曲目多为歌舞伴奏曲，有《阿妈勒火》《达娃雄奴》《耶几长木》等依百科与非遗资料
  • 地域风格流派：「按地理划分和不同地区歌舞风格，扎年琴形成了阿里风格 · 定日风格 · 拉孜风格 · 拉萨风格 · 贡布风格和安多风格等主要风格流派」依百科资料
  • 非遗与当代：「2008 年 6 月 7 日，藏族扎木聂弹唱经国务院批准列入第二批国家级非物质文化遗产名录」（申报地区：青海省海南藏族自治州 · 遗产编号 Ⅱ-131）；「2008 年，以扎年琴为主要伴奏乐器的拉孜堆谐被列入国家级非物质文化遗产名录」；「2014 年，扎年琴制作技艺入选第四批国家级非物质文化遗产项目名录」；「2026 年 7 月 26 日，青海省海南藏族自治州共和县组织了 626 名演奏者参与的『最大规模藏族扎木念弹唱』活动，并获得世界纪录认证」；「现代也有人改革札木聂」；「本世纪六十年代改革制成音箱大 · 蒙蟒皮的三种扎木聂：高音（全长 103 · 音箱长 27 厘米）· 中音（全长 111 · 音箱长 27 厘米）· 低音（全长 126 · 音箱长 48 厘米）」依非遗与百科资料
