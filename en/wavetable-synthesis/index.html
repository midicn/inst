Wavetable synthesis

Sound evolves before it sounds — timbre as a walk through an entire gallery

The previous page's sampler recorded the world — this page's instrument says: don't record, let the sound grow.

「Subtractive synthesis changes the sound after it's created. Wavetable synthesis changes the sound as it's being created.」

⭐⭐ This page is wavetable synthesis — third of the digital-synth family. One line: sound evolves before it sounds.

⭐⭐ The main line: three synthesis methods, three senses of space

「Subtractive synthesis: Carving one block of marble. FM: Two sculptors changing each other's work. Wavetable: Walking through an entire art gallery.」

⭐⭐ One of the loveliest analogies on the site — three methods in one breath:

MethodSpaceOne line
Subtractiveone marble blockcarving from a harmonic-rich wave
FMtwo sculptors editingoperators modulating each other
⭐⭐ Wavetablean entire gallerystrolling among hundreds of waveforms

⭐⭐ 「减法合成在声音被创造后改变它；波表合成在声音被创造时就改变它」— wavetable's evolution happens 「before sounding」, the fundamental difference from every earlier method.

⭐⭐ The principle: a book metaphor

「想象一本书，每一页都有不同的波形。慢慢翻页，声音不变；快速翻页或跳页，声音就变」.

⭐⭐ Technically: 「波表合成在存储器中存储一序列单周期波形，让演奏者或调制系统步进它们」; 「动态频谱移动通过扫描波形实现，用插值避免相邻波形间的明显跳变」— ⭐ interpolation is the technical name for 「walking without stumbling」: pages glide, not jump.

⭐⭐ Wavetable modulation: motion creates emotion

「几乎任何东西都能推动波表位置：LFO · 包络 · 力度 · 触后 · 弯音轮 · automation」; 「想象按住一个钢琴键……音色慢慢从：柔 → 亮 → 金属 → 攻击 → 空气——全部来自一个持续音」.

⭐⭐ 「运动创造情感。不再有静态声音——你得到的是演化的声音」— much of modern EDM's sound leans on wavetable movement.

⭐⭐ The folk mirror: 《哪得泥鳅变成龙》 — a loach becoming a dragon within one sustained note — the audible version of wavetable position moving.

⭐⭐ Software ran twenty years ahead of hardware

「波表合成首次用于 MUSIC-II——Max Vernon Mathews 1958 年开发的音色设计程序」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's 「lab before stage」 pattern: FM in a university（Chowning）· sampling in a radio station（El-Dabh）· wavetable in a Bell-Labs-style program（Mathews）— ⭐⭐ every breakthrough in electronic instruments happened first 「where there were no instruments」.

⭐⭐ The Wavecomputer 360: the lesson of no filter

「Palm 的努力产生了 Wavecomputer 360（1978）——30 张不同波表，每张 64 个波形」; 「滤波器的缺席使 360 听起来 buzz 又薄——这削弱了它最初商业可行性」.

⭐⭐ Against the Minimoog（pre-wiring for ease）and the ARP（pre-wired + overridable）— the Wavecomputer's lesson was 「pure digital is too刺」: ⭐⭐ wavetable's evolution needed an analog filter to 「close」 it.

⭐⭐ The PPG Wave series: the hybrid victory

「Wave 系列是前身的进化：数字声音引擎 + 模拟 VCA 与 24dB/oct VCF」; 「Wave 2（1981）→ Wave 2.2（1982 · twin modulation wheels）→ Wave 2.3（1984 · 8-part multitimbral）」; 「控制面板皆以独特的超群蓝 RAL 5002 着色」.

⭐⭐ 「数字振荡 + 模拟滤波」— the mirror of the Prophet-5（analog voice + digital control）: the Prophet was 「analog sound + digital memory」; the PPG was 「digital oscillation + analog close」— two ways of assembling old and new.

⭐⭐ The PPG Wave System: assembling against the Fairlight

「可连 PRK 智能键盘 · EVU 8 音扩展 · Waveterm A/B 波计算机——用于采样、音频操作、编辑、音序与创建用户自定义波表」; 「这套组合意在与更贵的 Fairlight CMI 竞争」.

⭐⭐ Against the samplers page: the Fairlight was one expensive machine; the PPG was an assemblable middle-class setup — two democratisation roads of the same era.（And like the Fairlight: PPG closed in 1987.）

⭐⭐ The player lineage: half the wall of synth-pop

「a-ha · Bowie · Depeche Mode · Gary Numan · Jarre · Art of Noise · Rush · Tangerine Dream · Tears for Fears · Dolby · Ultravox · Stevie Wonder · Trevor Horn……」

⭐⭐ Half the wall of synth-pop and the Berlin School is on that list — wavetable's 「evolving texture」 was exactly the 「future-feel」 the decade wanted.

⭐⭐ Vector synthesis: the neighbour's method（and a talent migration）

「矢量合成由 Sequential 以 Prophet VS 引入——把不同音色分配到正方形四角，用摇杆直观混合——声音可用坐标平面上的一个点表示」.

⭐⭐ The difference: 「矢量基于坐标平面位置算四声源的音量平衡；波表在单个波形周期内算不同波形的振幅比」— one is 「push-pull between four corners of a room」; the other is 「a stroll through a gallery」.

⭐⭐ The talent line: 「后来 Sequential 被 Yamaha 收购，开发团队转投 Korg—— 于是有了 Yamaha SY22 与 Korg Wavestation」— against the Prophet-5 company history: people leave, methods follow.

⭐⭐ Waldorf carried on, and the modern revival

「PPG 1987 年关闭，但波表原理通过 Waldorf Microwave 家族与大量现代软件合成器延续（部分与 Palm 本人合作开发）」; 「Waldorf Wave——今天被视为有史以来最受追捧的合成器之一」; 「没有 PPG 与 Waldorf，现代波表合成可能不存在」.

⭐⭐ Today: 「Xfer Serum 可能是世界上最流行的波表合成器 · Ableton Wavetable · 免费 Vital」— nearly every modern genre — EDM · Dubstep · Cinematic · Synthwave — wherever you hear evolving digital textures, wavetable synthesis is likely involved.
