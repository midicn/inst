The dizi's two schools and its playing techniques

South school trill-dou赠-hit, north school tong-slide-chuo-flower-fly — one flute, two wholly different voices

Fourteen aerophones are done in this section. But for the dizi, saying "it has a reed membrane" is not enough —

it is really two instruments: one south of the Yangtze, one north of the wall.

「竹笛技巧按门派分为南派和北派，⭐ 南派讲究叠、打、震、颤，北派讲究吐、剁、滑、花。」

⭐⭐ The better-known pair of lists is: south 颤·叠·赠·打, north 吐·滑·垛·花·飞.

⭐⭐ The dividing line: the south has no detached notes at all, the north is nearly all detached

Put the two lists side by side and the key point surfaces:

⭐ South · qudi (Jiangnan)⭐ North · bandi (north of the wall)
Techniques颤 · 叠 · 赠 · 打吐 · 滑 · 垛 · 花 · 飞
⭐⭐ How many are detached⭐⭐ none⭐⭐ only one (滑) is not
How it is played⭐ no tongue at all — one unbroken line⭐ the tongue is the busiest part
Charactergraceful · flowing · relaxedardent · ringing · penetrating
Musical roots⭐ kunqu + Jiangnan sizhu（followed for over 600 years）⭐ xintianyou + hua'er + labour calls
RepertoireZhao Songting《鹧鸪飞》Feng Zicun《喜相逢》(1953) ·《五梆子》《枣园春色》

⭐⭐ So the difference is not which notes exist but what the tongue does. The south barely moves the tongue and spends everything on breath and fingers; the north is the other way round — the tongue is the hardest-working part.

⭐⭐ The physical root: one tube's thickness

This is not a stylistic preference — the instrument decides it:

South · qudi — named for accompanying kunqu, and for being made in Suzhou, hence 「苏笛」. The body is thick and long: ⭐ the air fills a long tube, shaking the membrane into an airy, clear tone.

North · bandi — named for accompanying bangzi opera, nicknamed 「小笛子」. The body is short and thin: ⭐ because it suits a thin, translucent membrane, the vibration frequency rises and the tone becomes naturally bright and high.

⭐⭐ In one line: a thick tube → the air travels far → airy tone; a thin tube → more vibrations → bright tone. Which flute you pick is which school you have chosen.

⭐⭐ The south's four: none of them takes time

They share one property, and saying it first is clearer than explaining each:

⭐⭐ 「叠音、打音、赠音三者共同具有不占时值、附属性的特点」 — they are little tails hung off the main note, not notes in themselves.

颤音 — in the southern repertoire it is 「more free of rhythm and pitch than anywhere else」, used for free introductions and codas（⭐ i.e. the first breath and the last breath）. Besides the finger trill there are two more: 虚指颤音（⭐ the finger shakes without fully covering the hole, i.e. half-holing）and arm trill.

叠音 — 「layering the main note, the lowest finger lifts up toward the note above」; that is a second. ⭐ But by circumstance it may be a third or a fourth. ⭐ Its use is very specific: when two or more identical notes occur, the dou fills the gap.

打音 — 「strike downward quickly and lift, a downward interval of a second」.

赠音 — 「an extremely short, extremely fast, almost-not-there small tail note after a long note or the end of a phrase」.

⭐⭐ dou, da and zeng all take no time — which is exactly why the south sounds like one line: the notes are the skeleton, and these three are only decoration, and decoration costs no time.

And in《鹧鸪飞》Zhao Songting takes it to its limit: ⭐⭐ the decorative notes「替代了传统吐音系统，弱化音头」, in line with the southern principle of playing without the tongue; ⭐⭐ and within any single bar the decorative techniques stay within three, so the melody keeps the foreground（⭐ that discipline is real craftsmanship — however much decoration, the tune must stay the tune）.

⭐⭐ The north's five: each one imitates a sound from daily life

These five characters are ⭐⭐ not abstractions — each stands for something you can hear:

吐 (single, double and triple) 「through breath and tongue together, the tip taps forward, making a tu between teeth and lips, so the tone has grain and force」 ⭐ double tonguing = 「the first sounds tu, the second ku — together they make『突库』」, and it appears in long runs of sixteenth notes, colouring the atmosphere in a fast, cheerful movement ⭐⭐ triple tonguing = 「often 前八后十六 as『突突库』or 前十六后八 as『突库突』, used to depict soldiers advancing and horses galloping」 → ⭐⭐ a rhythm pattern is a picture: a galloping horse is triple tonguing

垛音 「built from a grace note into the main note, demanding a high explosive force — the tongue squeezes the air fast while the fingers press, producing a loud, penetrating sound」

滑音 ⭐⭐ paired against chuo, this is the most telling comparison on the page:

「垛音由装饰音和主干音构成…需要强爆发力」vs「滑音虽同样从装饰音过渡至主干音，但无需强爆发力，手指通过揉动实现几度音程的平滑过渡，音色圆润无痕，呈现出诙谐风趣的艺术效果」

⭐⭐ The same move — grace note into main note — is done by burst or by揉动: one is a shout, the other is a joke.

花舌 — 「the tongue vibrates of its own accord, giving the note a dense grain」

飞指 — 「using the arm to drive it, one finger or several (up to three) sweeps rapidly to and fro across one or more holes」, ⭐ producing a strong broken-sound effect, usually against a long note with changing dynamics, ⭐ or imitating a bird in flight

And what do the five do together? Feng Zicun 「运用『吐·滑·垛·花·飞』模仿人们聊天与欢笑， as far as possible 贴近北方人们奔放热闹的生活场景，注入真情实感」.

⭐⭐ His technique earned the nickname「吹破天」. But these five are not for blowing anything apart — ⭐⭐ they correspond to: conversation (double tonguing's tu-ku), laughter (flower tongue), a galloping horse (triple tonguing's rhythm), a bird in flight (flying fingers). ⭐⭐ Moving the sounds of daily life onto a flute — that is the northern school.

⭐⭐ An overlooked practical line: register decides how you breathe

It applies to both schools and is extremely useful:

⭐⭐ 「第三孔中音（同音作 5 的中音 1）以下的音区，以胸式呼吸为主, with the proportion rising

toward 100% as the note descends; 第三孔中音往上开始以腹式呼吸为主, rising from 50% to 100% by the top note.」

⭐⭐ Why? ⭐ 「若不采用（混合呼吸），则吹到第三孔中音时发音吃力、音色浑浊，往上则会吹不响或杂音过多；如果整个曲子采用腹式呼吸，则声音听起来生硬单调、共鸣感不强，且容易疲劳」

⭐⭐ So: the low register runs on chest (lots of air, steady), the high register on belly (fine, even). That is not a stylistic rule but a physical one — low notes want air, high notes want evenness.

⭐⭐ And one tip that saves breath: 「风门恰当地收缩，可以节省吹奏气息，这是熟练运用循环换气技巧的方法之一」 — ⭐⭐ for circular breathing the key is not the lungs but whether the wind-gate can narrow.

⭐⭐ Twenty lines of mnemonics (Zhao Songting,《笛艺春秋》)

The most directly usable passage on this page, because every line names a checkable action:

「风门正，笛身平，头要直，胸要挺。拇指小指相扶持，莫使笛子柳摇金。 虎口之间可容蛋，关节舒畅手才灵。六指按孔要严密，指指放松成弧形。 高低起落有规格，多余动作丢干净。口劲松紧随我意，口风粗细由我心。 双臂张开四十五，青龙白虎全都行。演奏姿态忌做作，严肃认真见真情。」

⭐⭐ Three of the most concrete: · 「虎口之间可容蛋」— ⭐ if an egg fits in the hand loop, the hand is relaxed（grip it tight and the fingers die） · 「高低起落有规格」— ⭐ fingers lift 1.5–2 cm: too high slows you down, too low drops the pitch · 「多余动作丢干净」— ⭐ in a large piece the technique count is high, and extra motion steals attention

⭐⭐ And one line that ties pitch to the mouth: 「一般情况是下嘴唇压笛子吹孔 1/4 位置」，⭐⭐ 「嘴唇和吹孔成九十度直角；角度小于九十度音准偏低，稍大于九十度音准偏高」 — ⭐⭐ one of the few places on this site that attributes a sharp note directly to the angle of the lips.

  • ⭐ 气息与口腔（两派共用的一条总纲）：⭐⭐「吹奏笛子时候的呼吸方法…要讲究科学自然，符合生理规律，其次讲求均匀、持久和稳定有张力」；⭐ 「口腔宜打开，采用唱美声时候的口腔状态，可以保证足够气流和气速」；⭐⭐ 「风门恰当地收缩，可以节省吹奏气息，这是熟练运用循环换气技巧的方法之一」；⭐ 气震音「即气流均匀波动，形成声音高低起伏的艺术效果，可以是先平后震，或先震后平」
  • ⭐ 一口诀（赵松庭《笛艺春秋》）：⭐⭐ 「风门正，笛身平，头要直，胸要挺。拇指小指相扶持，莫使笛子柳摇金。虎口之间可容蛋，关节舒畅手才灵。六指按孔要严密，指指放松成弧形。高低起落有规格，多余动作丢干净。口劲松紧随我意，口风粗细由我心。双臂张开四十五，青龙白虎全都行。演奏姿态忌做作，严肃认真见真情」—— ⭐⭐ 二十句里每一句都对应一个可检查的动作，这是本页最可直接照做的一段依专题类资料
  • ⭐ 笛子本身的两个小知识（与技法直接相关）：⭐ 笛膜「一般是用芦苇膜做成的，经揉纹后取一小方块使用」，作用是「经过气流振动笛膜，便能发出清脆、明亮的声音」；⭐ 缠丝「使用丝弦，缠于笛身外面，共有21 道至 24 道，起保护笛身以免炸裂的作用」；⭐ 海底（笛脑）「由笛塞内沿至吹孔中心的一段笛身内膛，它阻止气流向上，使口风向下流动，集中发音」依专题与百科类资料
