The music workstation

One box carrying the whole production chain — the M1 and its 250,000 siblings

⭐⭐ The main line: one box carrying the whole production chain

「在 M1 之前，制作一首曲子需要单独的合成器、鼓机、效果机架和音序器。 Korg 把所有这些放进一台 61 键力度+触后键盘背后，定价在职业乐手够得着的范围」.

⭐⭐ 「What made the M1 revolutionary was not any single feature but the combination of everything in one box」—

⭐⭐ The folk mirror: 《搭篷号》 — raise one tent, and the synth, drum machine, effects and sequencer all live under it. The workstation's historical role was turning 「rig-building」 into 「unboxing」.

⭐⭐ AI synthesis / S&S: the industry-standard architecture

「Korg 扩展了 Sample & Synthesis 的理念——1986 年在 DSS-1 上正式实现：不用经典模拟减法合成的简单模拟波形，而是用泛音丰富的复杂数字采样作为源，再施加完整的减法合成处理：滤波、LFO、包络发生器、数字效果」; 「结果的声音丰富、多彩、自然」.

⭐⭐ 「S&S synthesis 以不同的名字被今天各大合成器厂商使用—— Roland 的 SuperNatural · Yamaha 的 AWM · Korg 的 HI——是近年的例子」—

⭐⭐ The M1's architecture became the template of the whole industry. Against the three roads' convergence: the DX7 computed spectra · the sampler recorded audio · the M1 used the 「recorded」 as the 「source of computation」 — sampling + subtraction = the skeleton of every workstation since.

⭐⭐ 250,000 units and a company saved

「六年生产周期卖出约 250,000 台——M1 成为 Korg 直到 Triton 发布前最成功的合成器」; 「Outselling the Yamaha DX7 and Roland D-50」; 「M1 的巨大销量让 Korg 高管得以买回 Yamaha 持有的公司股份」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's company histories: Smith sold the Prophet company · Moog sold · Fairlight went bankrupt — ⭐⭐ the M1 is the rare case where 「building the best-selling synth」 actually saved the company. （And after the buyback, Yamaha still made Korg's keybeds — commerce and engineering each to its own.）

⭐⭐ The blown bottle: sound design in the sampling era

「独特的 blown bottle 声音由 Athan Billias 在一个大清酒瓶上吹潘笛采样而来—— 给乐器库添加空灵有机的质感」.

⭐⭐ Against the Fairlight's light pen（drawing sound by hand）— ⭐⭐ sound design in the sampling era was not turning knobs; it was hunting the world for sound — not sparing the sake bottle. 《手拿钥匙开铜箱》: inside the bronze box was a timbre blown across a sake bottle.

⭐ The 4MB chest

「4MB PCM 波形 ROM（144 个多采样音色）· 16 声部 · 8 部多音色 · 8 轨音序器（10 songs · 100 patterns · 7,700 音符）· 双数字效果处理器（混响/延迟/合唱/EQ/过载/旋转喇叭）」; 「滤波无共鸣——『对戏剧性滤波的需求被采样库的宽泛削弱了』」.

⭐⭐ 「M1 的内部 4MB 波形 ROM 以 1988 年的标准是巨量—— 当时台式 PC 的典型内存是 512 或 640 KB」— one synthesizer's sound library outweighed a whole computer's memory. 《手拿钥匙开铜箱》: the key is those 61 keys.

⭐⭐ World sounds as standard for the first time

「第一次，来自世界各地（尤其亚洲）的民族与异域音色作为标准提供」.

⭐⭐ Against the site: the M1 turned 「non-Western timbres」 from 「exotic exceptions」 into 「defaults of the library」 — a cultural step no smaller than the technical one.

⭐⭐ The two presets that ruled house and rave

「M1 的两个预设被 90 年代 house 与 rave 音乐大量使用：『Piano16』与『Organ2』」; 「Madonna 的 Vogue · Queen 的 Don't Try So Hard · 后来的 Beyoncé 与 Calvin Harris」.

⭐⭐ Against the DX7's E.PIANO 1（40% of 1986's number-one singles）— every flagship synth owns one or two presets that rule the dancefloor. 「Presets are distribution」（the dx7-fm page）replayed on the M1: timbre copied like a record.

⭐⭐ Lineage and software rebirth

「M1 一直生产到 1995 年底——比它的后继 T 系列停产还久」; 「Trinity → Triton（1999）→ Kronos」; 「Korg Legacy Collection 与 iM1 app 软件复刻」.

⭐⭐ An unbroken bloodline from 1988 to today — the workstation became the standard form of the digital instrument: first the box, then the cloud.
