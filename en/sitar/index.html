The sitar

Named for three strings, fitted with six or seven — and its frets are a block of wood that slides

1 min read459 characters条目World InstrumentsUpdated 2026-10-21

The sitar's frets are not wire nailed to a board. Each is an arched block of wood, twenty or so in a row, sliding along the fingerboard.

Push one and the string's speaking length changes, and the note moves with it. Its intonation is not fixed at the factory; the player sets it before playing.

A fret you can push

Elsewhere the frets are pinned in a row and the player chooses among boxes. The sitar turns the box itself into an action: intonation stops being fixed and becomes adjustable on the spot.

The oud abandons frets; the santur has a movable bridge but fixed frets. The sitar does both — movable frets and a movable bridge.

The name says three, the instrument has six or seven

The name is Persian setar: se is three, tar is string — a three-stringed instrument. A modern sitar has one to four melody strings plus two to five drone strings, six or seven in all.

The name stopped at the most typical shape while the instrument moved on. It is a three-stringed instrument and a self-accompanying one: the melody stops, the drone keeps going.

A leather ring, baddhi, ties at the bridge so the string still vibrates slightly above it: the note neither muffled nor bright.

A raga is a rule, not a scale

Twelve ragas make up the Hindustani modal system. A thaat is the parent seven-note scale; a raga is one way of walking it — the same notes climb and fall by entirely different routes in different ragas. A raga is a rule, not a list.

The right thumb wears a metal guard to pluck; the left hand draws meend, gamak, hasta and murki out of the string. That is not decoration added to a melody; it is pitch being drawn.

The 1968 tour

The White Album tour of Ravi Shankar and George Harrison carried the sitar into global pop culture. Other instruments travelled with trade, empire and factories; this one was carried by a British guitarist touring with an Indian player.

The name has another layer: in Sanskrit sitar is the word for woman, related to mother. An instrument carrying its mother tongue entered the English-speaking world.

Today it shares a North Indian classical stage with violin, sarangi and tabla: melody to the strings, rhythm to the drum, and the middle left to the singer.

HS class

HS class: Chordophone · plucked · necked bowl lute (321.321) · definite pitch.

  • 对照线 —— oud（乌德琴）： HS 321.321-6 与西塔琴同科， 编号完全相同这件事本身就是一条历史证据（对照「出口后的变种」：乌德琴出欧洲加品格 → 鲁特琴）；santur（桑图尔）： 同一条解法的两件琴 —— 桑图尔改的是弦下的桥， 西塔琴改的是弦上那块可以推着走的木料， 一家把音准做成调音时定死的东西， 另一家把它交还给演奏者；cimbalom（钦巴龙）： 桑图尔那条路线的机械化终点（桥加踏板加全半音）；qanun（卡农琴）： 在演奏中改音高的那一种（铜片杠杆压弦）， 与西塔琴推一下琴码就换一格的即时性最接近；tanbur（弹布尔）· dutar（都塔尔）· dombra（冬不拉）： 亚洲长颈拨弦的邻居， 它们的品是缠在指板上的丝弦， 一改几何就改不动的琴；violin（小提琴）： 在北印度古典音乐里与小提琴、萨朗吉、塔布拉同台 —— 小提琴有旋律但音高固定， 塔布拉能自由调音却发不出旋律， 两种乐器各占一半；harmonium（簧风琴）： 北印度歌唱伴奏里的键盘件， 与西塔琴、塔布拉一起构成「唱为主、琴为辅」的编制。
