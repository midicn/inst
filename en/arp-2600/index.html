The ARP 2600

Semi-modular — pre-wired but overridable, the third road that drove into high schools

1 min read453 characters条目ElectronicUpdated 2026-10-04

A 1971 synthesizer with dozens of jacks across the panel, and it makes a sound without a single cable plugged in. Press a key and the note is there.

The third road

A Moog modular had to be patched; unpatched it was silent. The Minimoog pulled all the wiring inside: switch on and go, but never change the routing. The ARP 2600 sat between them, a fixed set of modules pre-wired internally, with patch points left on the panel to override the defaults.

It is an instrument first, a modular system second. The learning path is on the front: hear the default, pull one cord, then wire your own. That is the order of most synth teaching today — preset, tweak, build a patch.

Classrooms, not music shops

At launch ARP sold the 2600 heavily to high schools, universities and other institutions. One generation of synthesizers, two routes: the Minimoog went to shops, the 2600 went to classrooms. Shops produced players; classrooms produced sound designers.

Ben Burtt came out of the second route. He built R2-D2's voice for Star Wars from a 2600 plus his own throat, and the sound of the Ark in Raiders of the Lost Ark came from the same machine. The 2600 has an external audio input. He plugged himself in.

Endorsements, a lawsuit, two keyboards

Alan R. Pearlman sold instruments the way he designed them. He gave synths to Edgar Winter, Pete Townshend, Stevie Wonder, Joe Zawinul and Herbie Hancock, and got a professional endorsement from each; Edgar Winter recorded Frankenstein on one. The Minimoog was discovered from below. ARP worked from above, letting the stars' sound do the advertising.

An early version copied Moog's four-pole ladder filter, drawing a legal threat that never became a suit. Joe Zawinul usually played two 2600s at once in Weather Report. To make a monophonic instrument sing two parts he did not modify the synth; he added a second one.

Reissues, and the quirk as moat

The 2600 went through three versions: the blue-grey Blue Marvin, named after ARP's financial officer Marvin Cohen and often misremembered as Blue Meanies; a wood-veneer case; a black aluminium panel with orange lettering.

In January 2020 Korg reissued it as the ARP 2600 FS, faithful to the 1972 grey version, in a limited run, adding two filter types, balanced XLR out, MIDI, a sequencer and arpeggiator, and a flight case. In November Behringer released its own variant: nineteen-inch rack, no speakers, LED faders, a digital spring-reverb emulation, switchable filters.

Because analog quirks make an exact digital copy nearly impossible. Three oscillators drift in their own ways, and a spring tank's bounce is not computed. The quirk is the moat.

  • 主线：半模块化 —— 预接线但可覆盖（本页最该记住的一条） —— 「与同时代其他需要单独购买模块并由用户接线的模块化系统不同，2600 是半模块化的——一组固定的基本合成器组件内部预接线」；「因此它可以接也可不接 patch 线来操作」→ 在「必须接线」（Moog 模块化）与「不能接线」（Minimoog）之间开出第三条路——预接的默认声音 + 可以覆盖的 patch 点，学习路径就刻在面板上
  • 教育市场（与 Minimoog 零售路线的分岔） —— 「首发时它被大量营销给高中、大学与其他教育机构」→ 对照 Minimoog 的「零售店路线」：同一代合成器，一个走进乐器行，一个走进教室——两条路都走通了，但走出的用户完全不同
  • 设计者与出身 —— 「由 Dennis Colin 为 Alan R. Pearlman 设计，ARP Instruments 生产，是 ARP 2500 的后继」；「1971–81 年生产」；「单音 · 3 VCO · 24dB/oct 低通 · 弹簧混响 · 49 键」；三个版本：「第一版 Blue Marvin（蓝灰金属箱 · ARP 幼年期在一间小厂房组装——名字来自当时的 CFO Marvin Cohen，常被误称 Blue Meanies）→ 贴面木箱+金属角（更耐用便携）→ 黑铝面板橙标（配合 ARP 黑/橙产品线）」
  • 法律故事：模仿 Moog 梯形滤波器 —— 「早期版本包含一个对 Robert Moog 的 4 极『梯形』VCF 的模仿——后来成为一场被威胁（最终并未发生）的诉讼」→ 与 voltage-control 条的「1V/oct 分歧」同族：滤波器电路也是厂商间的主权之争（对照梯形滤波器条目：「一件发明里最长寿的部分是一个部件」——长到别人要来争）
  • Pearlman 的营销创新：名人代言的先驱 —— 「Alan R. Pearlman 作为推销员与作为合成器设计师一样创新——他把合成器送给知名音乐家：Edgar Winter · Pete Townshend · Stevie Wonder · Joe Zawinul · Herbie Hancock——每位换取他作为专业用户的背书」→ 对照 Minimoog 的自下而上（爵士乐手自己发现）——ARP 是自上而下的代言；Edgar Winter 用它录了《Frankenstein》
  • R2-D2 与约柜（电影声音史的名场面） —— 「声音设计师 Ben Burtt 用 ARP 2600 结合他自己的声音，创造了《星球大战》里 R2-D2 的声音」；「Burtt 还用 2600 创造了《夺宝奇兵》中约柜的音效」→ 一台「教新手」的合成器，造出了影史最著名的机器人嗓音（对照特雷门的科幻配乐传统——ARP 2600 接过了「宇宙声」的接力棒）
  • Joe Zawinul 的一手一台 —— 「Joe Zawinul 在 Weather Report 通常同时弹两台 2600（一手一台）」——把「单音乐器」弹成双声部的方式：不是改造乐器，是复制乐手
  • 2020 双复刻 —— 「2020-01-10，Korg 复刻 ARP 2600 FS——忠实复刻 1972 灰版，一次性限量；新增两种滤波、平衡 XLR、MIDI、基础音序器/琶音器与飞行箱」；「2020-11-03 Behringer 发布自家变体（19 英寸机架 · 无扬声器 · LED 推子 · 数码弹簧混响仿真 · 两版滤波可切换）」；「模拟电路的怪癖使精确的数字复制品几乎不可能」（对照音轮风琴的「相位关系难以复制」——怪癖即护城河，第三次出现）
