The Hammond organ

Frequencies cast into metal bumps, tone pulled on drawbars — gospel on Sunday morning, jazz on Saturday night

Related

The last two pages wrote electronic instruments whose frequencies live in circuits.

This page's instrument casts its frequencies into metal — a gear, a ring of bumps.

「每个音轮边缘有一圈凸点；凸点的数量与轮的转速，决定该音的频率」.

⭐⭐ This page is the Hammond tonewheel organ — fourth in the electronic section. One line: the frequency is cast into metal.

⭐⭐ The main line: the gear casts the pitch into metal

The theremin's frequency lives in an oscillator circuit; the Martenot's too — the Hammond's lives in gears and bumps:

「同步电机通过轴与齿轮带动音轮——齿轮比决定每个轮的转速，整个齿轮系持续旋转。每只音轮旁有一只电磁拾取器，凸点掠过时磁场波动，在线圈里产生周期性电压——一个音」.

⭐⭐ Faster wheel, higher pitch; denser bumps, higher pitch — the frequency is not tuned, it is cast.

⭐⭐ A mirror of the electric-guitar pickup: the guitar is string moving, field moving; the Hammond is wheel moving, field moving — the same induction, with the string swapped for a gear.

⭐⭐ And taxonomically it is 「electromechanical」, not 「electronic」: 「波形由机械音轮而非电子振荡器产生」— ⭐ which is exactly why it files under 「early electronic」 rather than the electronic canon（one of this site's classification scruples）.

⭐⭐ Drawbars: mixing waves mid-performance — additive synthesis before the word

The pipe organ's tone lives in stops — chosen before playing. The Hammond moved the choice into performance:

「拉杆（drawbars）是双键盘上方的金属滑杆，像调音台的推子——拉出越多，该谐波越响；推到底音量归零。拉杆的命名源自管风琴的音栓系统——按音管的物理长度命名」.

⭐⭐ The drawbars mix the fundamental, the sub-octave and the overtones — 「designing the timbre」 moved from the organ's pre-chosen stops to live mixing.

⭐⭐ This is additive synthesis before the textbooks: 「模仿管风琴多组音管，用谐波序列波形的加法合成生成声音」— chapter one of the synthesizer manuals was played on this organ for thirty years.

⭐⭐ The B-3's drawbars are its secret weapon: 「每手九根拉杆实时混合谐波——拉出前两根是温暖如笛的爵士音；九根全拉就是尖叫般谐波复杂的摇滚音」.

⭐⭐ Key click: the third 「flaw into signature」

「那个独特的 key click（按键咔嗒声）原本是设计缺陷，迅速成为『Hammond 音色』的一部分，现代仿制品努力复现它」.

⭐⭐ Third time across this site: Ted Nugent's feedback（electric guitar）· the Martenot's lateral keys（vibrato）· the Hammond's key click — a flaw becoming a signature is close to a law. ⭐ And this one is the most thorough: the flaw became something people now pay to buy back.

⭐⭐ The Leslie: a rival becomes standard equipment

A rare market comedy in instrument history:

「Don Leslie 设计的扬声器被广泛用于 Hammond 风琴——尽管 Leslie 起初是 Hammond 想挤垮的竞争公司」.

⭐⭐ Where the Leslie sound comes from: 「旋转的喇叭与带旋转挡板的固定低音扬声器，产生颤音效果」— the Doppler effect moved into a speaker cabinet; 「电子管功放给 Hammond 一种温暖自然的过载——从轻微『呼噜』到重度咆哮」.

⭐⭐ 「很快，Leslie 音箱成了 Hammond 玩家的 de facto 必备」— the complete voice of the organ = the instrument + the rival's speaker it once tried to destroy.

⭐⭐ The market: church → jazz → rock

「最初卖给教堂，作为管风琴的低价替代」.

⭐⭐ But jazz players found another life: 「它很快受到职业爵士乐手欢迎——organ trio； 爵士俱乐部老板发现 organ trio 比请大乐队便宜」.

⭐⭐ Jimmy Smith's B-3 changed everything: 「他的演奏单枪匹马确立了爵士风琴三重奏这一主要形式」; through the 60s–70s into R&B · rock（especially prog）· reggae.

⭐⭐ Two deities on the rock side: Keith Emerson — 「把 Hammond 演奏变成体育竞技，在台上真的动手打乐器」; Jon Lord（Deep Purple） — 「把 C-3 接进 Marshall 音箱，咆哮着与吉他竞争」.

⭐⭐ One line: 「Hammond 是周日早上与周六晚上的声音——福音的狂喜与爵士的老练、摇滚的放纵与灵魂乐的砂砾」（⭐ beside the theremin's「eerie」and the Martenot's「lyrical」: the Hammond's character is 「fervent」）.

⭐ Why the pedalboard has only 25 notes

「Hammond 用 25 键脚踏板是因为他发现——传统 32 键教堂踏板的最上七个音很少被用到」.

⭐⭐ Leaving out the parts nobody used — a kindness only engineers do （the 32-note AGO board survives only on the Concert models）.

⭐ One more detail: 「手键盘与踏板最初用钯合金线制造，保证按键电连接—— 音轮风琴含 3.2–8.4 克钯」— ⭐ a pinch of precious metal hiding in the instrument.

⭐⭐ Decline and resurrection

「1975 年放弃音轮转向集成电路，销量下滑，公司 1985 年倒闭」; the name was bought by Suzuki — 2002's「New B-3」recreates the original digitally.

⭐⭐ Clonewheel instruments — Korg CX3 · Roland VK-7 · Nord Electro — the name itself is an homage; 「用现代软件仿真音轮 Hammond 的声音是困难的，因为音轮之间的相位关系难以复制」.

⭐⭐ The instrument's 「flaw」（gear phase relationships）is the one thing even digitisation cannot copy — mechanical imperfection became its deepest moat.
