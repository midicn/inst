The suona's regional schools

The shape of one reed decides the voice of a whole school

Related

SuonaDizi schools and techniquesGuanziTimbreRegister

Fourteen aerophones are done in this section, and most of them are sorted by *form* — large, middle, small, reed or no reed.

Only the suona is sorted by the local shape of its reed.

「东北唢呐的哨片呈扇面形状，因而音色亮；而山东唢呐的哨片呈布袋形，吹出的声音也因此相对柔和。」

⭐⭐ The main line: three schools, three reeds, three voices

Put the three sentences together and the causal chain is complete:

SchoolReed shapeVoice
⭐ Northeastfan-shaped⭐ bright
⭐ Shandongbag-shaped⭐ comparatively soft
⭐ Henanfan-shaped, wide opening「不易控制，发音高亢响亮」

⭐⭐ The reed is the one part that cannot be standardised — 「手工制作的哨片成功率不及百分之十，即使是经验丰富的工匠，也不敢在制作过程中掉以轻心」.

⭐⭐ So whichever village's makers produce whichever reed, that village grows its own school.

The five schools in one line: 「以汉族唢呐为例，有粗犷浑厚的东北派、清脆明亮的山东派、热情朴质的河北派、厚重有力的陕北派和柔润风雅的潮州派」.

⭐⭐ Schools subdivide again: 「流派内部又会因演奏大师风格不同，再形成小流派——比如在安徽，民间唢呐就根据隋兰魁、张连升、刘凤鸣三位唢呐大师的演奏风格，在当地形成三个流派」.

⭐⭐ Each school's signature: all of them imitate something else

The most interesting thing here: not one school's signature technique is "make the suona sound like a suona" —

School⭐ SignatureImitates
⭐ Hebei箫音 · 三弦音⭐⭐ the suona imitating a xiao and a sanxian — 「这两种技法是河北流派的绝技」; 「弱奏时犹如洞箫之声」
⭐ Henanplaying opera⭐⭐ the human singing voice — 「旋律如人声唱腔——自如流畅、风趣幽默、韵味浓郁」
⭐ Shandong气吐音 · 弹舌音 · 气拱音 · 反弹舌音dozens of techniques that 「为乐曲增添了华丽的色彩」
⭐ Northeastthe sound made with the teeth biting the lips⭐⭐ Wang Qingzhong: 「东北的唢呐风格主要是豪迈，它特有的演奏技巧是用牙齿咬着嘴唇发出的声音」
⭐ Chaozhouslide · single tonguing · finger trill「音乐非常细腻、委婉……表现情感比较内在、含蓄」

⭐⭐ The Hebei line is worth a pause: the loudest instrument in the folk ensemble, and its highest art is to sound like a xiao. The suona is the loudest wind in the village — 「军听了军愁，民听了民怕」— ⭐⭐ and its supreme skill is to quiet itself down. One of the loveliest paradoxes in organology.

⭐⭐ Henan: playing opera grew a whole set of instruments

Henan has the richest material, because opera is its lifeblood:

「河南派唢呐受到豫剧、越调、二夹弦影响非常大，在河南派唢呐演奏当中 ⭐⭐ 吹戏也成为检验唢呐吹手的重要能力。」

⭐⭐ To play opera, Henan grew two instruments of its own:

Nazi（folk name「短家伙」）— 「主要由一个铜管芯子和一个类似唢呐哨片的大号哨子组成」: · the large nazi sounds low → imitates the painted-face and black-face roles（《包青天》《打金枝》） · the small nazi sounds high → imitates the female and young-male roles（《花木兰》《穆桂英挂帅》）

Mendi — 「竹子做的，音孔和唢呐一样有八个……发出来的声音柔和、憨厚，音色感觉上像管子」.

⭐⭐ One school, in pursuit of the human voice, branched into three instruments.

⭐⭐ And an identity-level detail: Henan carries a signature pitch 「♯4」— 「这个音在吹奏的时候要略低于十二平均律的『♯4』，音高在『4』与『♯4』之间」.

⭐⭐ A deliberately "wrong" note became the school's identity marker.

Henan further splits into four sub-schools（each with its own repertoire）: Yubei（Anyang: 《将军令》《对胡儿》）· Yuxi（《过街俏》《大钉缸》）· Yudong（Kaifeng:《大开门》《小开门》, 「委婉、柔和」）· Yunan（Xuchang & Zhumadian:《云里游》《天下同》,「细腻、质朴」）.

And the band's status is written into the wedding proverbs of Qinyang: 「大花轿、麻金吹，麻金不吹不结婚」「闺姑女、门婿到，毛旦不吹不上轿」.

⭐⭐ The band's name entered the preconditions of marriage itself.

⭐⭐ The Minxi pair: one male, one female

A rare scoring anywhere on this site:

「通常两支一起合吹列奏，称为『公吹』和『嫲吹』，构造相同，但长度和粗细有别， 『公』长『嫲』短，『公』粗『嫲』细，『公吹』的音色雄壮，『嫲吹』的发音空灵」。

⭐⭐ Two suonas are not "one after the other" — they are a pair, male and female — ⭐ the paired tradition shared with Hubei's witch-music（also two suonas）.

⭐⭐ The Dazhu bamboo suona: not one piece of metal

The extreme case of local materials:

「数百年前，唢呐传入巴蜀，大竹人就地取材，用本地的竹子制作了唢呐」— 「哨由大竹县当地燕麦秆做成，杆子采用大竹县特产的罗汉竹制作，喇叭则由当地产的黄竹篾丝编织成形后，再刮灰涂上土漆」.

⭐⭐ Oat-straw reed, luohan-bamboo body, huangzhu-woven bell — not one piece of metal.

⭐ Compare the standard suona（reed reed + wood body + bronze bell）: every material is different, and the instrument still works — ⭐⭐ which shows the suona's "self" is not its materials but the structure: a conical tube + a double reed + a bell.

⭐⭐ The 1993 live-core: putting the adjustable part into the hand

「1993 年，由著名管乐演奏家郭雅志先生发明了唢呐『活芯』装置，令传统唢呐奏出半音阶、十二音体系」.

⭐⭐ How it works: 「传统的芯子为单管式，而活芯为活动套式，可以伸缩改变长度…… 演奏时，通过双手向上压进活芯，便能奏出临时变化半音。如果把活芯推进、卡定，即可做高半音的移调乐器使用，指法不变」.

⭐⭐ Compare the niutuiqin: its sound post is turned by hand; the suona's live core is pressed by hand — ⭐⭐ two unrelated instruments both handed the "adjustable part" to the player's hands rather than to the factory.

⭐⭐ The Ming six-line indictment

The best writing on the suona is an attack on it:

「喇叭，唢呐，曲儿小，腔儿大。官船来往乱如麻，全仗你抬身价。军听了军愁，民听了民怕，哪里去辨什么真共假？眼见得吹翻了这家，吹伤了那家，只吹得水尽鹅飞罢。」 — Ming · Wang Pan,《朝天子·咏喇叭》

⭐⭐ 「曲儿小，腔儿大」— five characters, the site's most vivid summary of loudness.

⭐⭐ And 「军听了军愁，民听了民怕」— an instrument so loud it became the sound of power.

⭐ Six lines that capture an instrument's entire social energy — the sharpest passage in this site's sources.

which is why it split into so many schools: everywhere it went, it imitated whatever lived there.
