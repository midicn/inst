The digital piano

Two technical lines converging — the Rhodes on stage, the Clavinova in the living room

⭐⭐ The main line: one name, two utterly different instruments

「Electric Piano（机电电钢琴）：靠琴槌击金属弦/音叉/簧片，经拾音器放大出声—— 有机械结构、有物理振动，是早期舞台电钢。 Digital Piano（数码钢琴）：纯电子发声，采样真钢琴音色，靠芯片运算 + 扬声器回放，无机械振动，是现在主流家用/教学款」.

⭐⭐ The confusion of the name is itself a piece of history — 「electric piano」 referred, in sequence, to two fundamentally different instruments.

⭐⭐ The electromechanical era: from Neo-Bechstein to Rhodes

「Neo-Bechstein 电钢琴问世，由贝希斯坦 + 诺贝尔得主能斯特 + 西门子联合开发— 核心原理：缩短琴弦，用拾音器把振动转电信号再放大」.

⭐⭐ The three classics of the golden age:

⭐ Fender Rhodes⭐ WurlitzerYamaha CP
Structuretinereedhammer action（near-acoustic）
Tonewarm, hazybrighter, snappier（bites when overdriven）stage-real acoustic

⭐⭐ 「bell-like timbre and touch sensitivity made it the first electric keyboard to truly rival acoustic piano expressiveness」 — the Rhodes was the first electric keyboard that could truly rival the acoustic.

⭐⭐ But the shared limits were written into the gig: 「有机电音色、必须接音箱、重、维护麻烦，不能当练琴主力」— 「无法还原真钢手感，不适合儿童启蒙与考级练习」 — ⭐⭐ 《行过山路记艰难》: the roads the Rhodes family walked were all hardship — and the digital piano solved exactly that hardship.

⭐⭐ The digital piano is born: the 1983 Clavinova

「1970s 末–1980s 初：PCM 采样技术成熟，开始录制真钢琴声音存入芯片」; 「1983：Yamaha 推出第一台真正的家用数码钢琴——数字采样技术 + 配重键盘」.

⭐⭐ The Clavinova's revolution in one line:

「The Yamaha Clavinova democratized piano playing by offering acoustic piano sounds without maintenance, tuning, or space requirements.」

⭐⭐ 「把钢琴敬进千家万户的客厅」 — the word 「敬奉」 in the folk title is the Clavinova's historical role.

⭐ 「Yamaha YP-30 通常被引为第一台数码钢琴（1970s 末）— the Clavinova（1983）was the one that popularised it.」

⭐⭐ Three evolutionary lines（the page's hardest passage）

Sound: 「机电振动拾音 → 模拟电路 → PCM 数字采样 → 多层采样 + 共鸣建模 → 音色从『电子味』到无限接近三角钢琴」; Touch: 「弹簧无配重 → 半配重 → 全配重 → 逐级配重 → 擒纵仿檀木键盘—— 从『玩具手感』到保护手型、适配考级」; Function: 「只能发声 → 接 MIDI → 蓝牙、录音、分轨、智能纠错、网课适配—— 从单一乐器变成练琴+教学+创作一体化工具」.

⭐⭐ Three lines advancing together made the digital piano truly 「like a piano」 — voice, touch, function: miss one and it is still a toy.

⭐⭐ Clavinova milestones: even the rival became a sound source

「1983 first model → 1990s advanced sampling → 2000s Graded Hammer Action → 2010s Virtual Resonance Modeling（VRM）→ 2020s CFX and Bösendorfer Imperial samples」.

⭐⭐ The latest generation samples 「another maker's flagship」（Bösendorfer）— the rival became the sound source（⭐ against the DX7 computing timbre with VLSI: the digital piano borrows it by sampling — borrowing from its own biggest rival）.

⭐⭐ The DX7's E.PIANO 1, before and after

「E.PIANO 1——那个在录音里有效替代 Rhodes 的电钢琴 patch」（see the dx7-fm page）—

⭐⭐ the Rhodes sound was first 「computed」 into an FM preset, then 「recorded」 into a digital piano — two digitisations, one protagonist（victim or beneficiary）. The Rhodes' physical structure（tine + pickup）died, but its voice lives in two generations of digital instruments.

⭐⭐ Hybrid and silent: two roads in opposite directions

「hybrid pianos: acoustic mechanisms + advanced sampling — Kawai's flagship hybrid at $55,000」; 「Silent pianos: an acoustic action + a hammer bar stopping the hammers + headphones」; 「Yamaha Disklavier / Bösendorfer CEUS: solenoids and MIDI replacing the paper roll」.

⭐⭐ One road is 「electronic made real」（a hybrid with a true action inside）; the other is 「real made electronic」（a true piano with a silent system）— ⭐⭐ the roads meet in the middle: the endpoint is 「acoustic touch + electronic silence」.

⭐⭐ The China milestone（2001）

「2001 年高校扩招，电钢琴进入专业音乐教室，推动教学普及」; 「2015 年前后智能电钢爆发，适配网课、集体课、家庭练琴」.

⭐⭐ The digital piano's 「education popularisation」 has clear dates in China — the classroom of 2001, the online course of 2015, the everyman of the 2020s: three milestones, three generations of learners.
