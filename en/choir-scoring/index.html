Choir scoring

Scoring is another kind of architectural acoustics — the first stereo was two choirs in facing lofts

Related

⭐⭐ The main line: scoring is another kind of architectural acoustics

「this placement of performers and the specific writing of the parts created the first type of stereo sound and three-dimensional listening experiences」.

⭐⭐ Humanity's first stereo was two choirs standing in facing lofts — cori spezzati (split choirs) developed at St. Mark's Basilica in the sixteenth century: 「composers would contrast different instrumentalists and groups of singers utilizing the effects of space in the performance venue, that is, the church」. ⭐⭐ Space is an instrument; the building is the score.

⭐⭐ The democratisation of parts: the polyphonic revolution

⭐⭐ The essence of Netherlandish polyphony: 「treat each voice part as being of equal importance, rather than……one voice singing a slow cantus firmus, with other parts providing fluent, yet unimportant, contrapuntal accompaniments」 — ⭐⭐ from 「one voice holding a long note while the others accompany」 to 「every voice is a subject」 — the equality of parts arrived on paper three centuries before the equality of people.

⭐⭐ Sonata pian'e forte (1597)

「pioneering in musical scoring in that Gabrieli wrote specific louds and softs into the individual parts」.

⭐⭐ The p/f signs plus the pianoforte (crystallised by Cristofori around 1700) — the same semantic field growing at once on the part and the keyboard: expression became a writable parameter for the first time. (Against the vocal-production entry: dynamics were always a variable of singing; after 1597 they became an instruction in the score.)

⭐⭐ In ecclesiis (1615): a four-layer texture

⭐⭐ 「Gabrieli has three 『choirs』 at play: the soloists, the chorus, and the instruments, all supported by the organ」 plus continuo — ⭐⭐ soloists · chorus · instruments · organ: a four-layer scoring, the Baroque basilica's 「multitrack recording」; the continuo is the harmonic foundation, the soloist decorates upon it.

⭐⭐ Scoring is timbre design — headcount and part-count decide the harmonic density. (Against the voice-types entry: SATB is a taxonomy; the choir is the machine that turns the taxonomy into sound.)

⭐ The Chinese line and MIDI's loft

⭐⭐ The choralisation of call-and-response (the modern redemption of 「the lead is the proto-first voice」); Xian Xinghai's Yellow River Cantata (1939), text by Guang Weiran, made the chorus an institutional genre of modern China. ⭐⭐ Kam grand song (no conductor · a cappella · polyphonic) — polyphony without scoring: scoring is a Western invention, polyphony is humanity's shared property.

⭐⭐ MIDI's loft: the double choir's left-right placement → the twentieth century's pan knob and multitrack recorder → the DAW's automated panning — ⭐⭐ from St. Mark's lofts to Ableton's panning: cori spezzati's digital descendants turned 「space」 into a draggable parameter.
