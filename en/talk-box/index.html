The talk box

The guitar borrows your mouth as its filter — the most bodily effect of the electronic age

Related

The vocoderElectric guitarThereminTimbrePitch

⭐⭐ The main line: the filtering happens in the mouth

「A talk box is an effects device that allows a musician to modify the sound of a musical instrument. The musician controls the modification by changing the shape of their mouth」; 「The shape of the mouth changes the harmonic content of the sound in the same way it affects the harmonic content generated by the vocal folds when speaking」.

⭐⭐ The site's 「body as instrument」 lineage goes electric: the jaw harp relies on the mouth's resonance · throat-singing tunes the throat as a cavity · yodel switches registers — the talk box plugs this line into the electric age: the mouth makes no sound, the mouth filters. The guitar has no vocal cords. It borrows yours.

⭐⭐ The principle: a tube, three legs of the journey

LegWhat happens
① AmplifierThe guitar (or keyboard) amp output is routed into the floor pedal
② The pedalA horn driver inside the box (a speaker unit with its horn replaced by the tube connection) sounds, entering the plastic tube by an air-tight connection; a foot switch toggles between talk-box speaker and normal speaker
③ The mouthThe other end of the tube is taped by the microphone, reaching toward the mouth — the sound is directed into the performer's mouth; the mouth's shape (lips, tongue, palate, teeth) is the variable filter
④ MicrophoneThe 「shaped」 sound exits the mouth and enters the microphone — an instrument/voice hybrid is heard

⭐⭐ 「the performer can mouth words, with the resulting effect sounding as though the instrument is speaking」 — the only 「electrophone」 on this site that still sounds with the power off: tube in mouth, guitar stopped, the mouth keeps shaping the decay.

⭐⭐ The invention dispute: three claimants, one puppet

⭐⭐ 1944 · Alvino Rey's puppet: 「an early version of the talk box to record “St. Louis Blues” along with a puppet called “Stringy”」 — the puppet's mouth moved while a human blew the tube offstage — the talk box's ancestor is puppet theatre. (Against the site's unplanned-invention lineage: the talking puppet came twenty years before the talking guitar.)

ClaimantYearEvidence
Pete Drake1964the album *Forever*'s 「talking steel guitar」 — an 8-inch paper-cone speaker joined to a tube
Doug Forbes / Kustom1969「The Bag」 — the first mass-produced talk box; 30W driver; shoulder bag like a leather water bladder
Bob Heil1971built for Joe Walsh's Barnstorm tour — 250W JBL driver + hi-pass filter

⭐⭐ Forbes states the same concept had previously been patented as an artificial larynx — ⭐⭐ the talk box's direct ancestor is a medical device: the tube that gave the voiceless back their 「speech」. Instrument history borrowed from medicine one thing — and then let the guitar talk. (Against the site's name-vs-reality lineage: saer's shifted name, the synthesizer's 1929 instrument — three parties fight over the talk box's name; the thing itself may belong to none of them.)

⭐⭐ The Frampton myth: a Christmas present and a decade

「In 1973, Heil gave his talk box to Peter Frampton as a Christmas present — a 100-watt hand-built fibre-glass version」; 「Due to the success of the album Frampton Comes Alive!, and particularly the hit singles “Do You Feel Like We Do” and “Show Me the Way”, Frampton has become somewhat synonymous with the talk box」 — it is even called a framptone.

⭐⭐ 「it's rumoured that Frampton locked himself away for two weeks to master the new effect」 — against the LinnDrum entry's quantize/swing: one 「frustration → invention」, one 「practice → apotheosis」. The talk box's difficulty is all in the mouth: speaking clearly around a tube is the mouth's extreme sport.

⭐⭐ In 1988 Heil sold the manufacturing rights to Dunlop — which still builds it 「to the exact same specifications」. Against the site's company histories (Moog sold, Sequential went bankrupt, Prophet was bought back): Heil sold the specification; the specification lived — another way an instrument outlives its company.

⭐ The other branch: funk's machine voice

⭐⭐ Roger Troutman, lead singer of Zapp, was well known for using a talk box — “More Bounce to the Ounce” — the West Coast G-funk lineage (T-Pain · Snoop Dogg) starts here. ⭐⭐ Two roads to the machine voice: Kraftwerk's machine voice ran on electricity (the vocoder's filter banks); Zapp's ran on the mouth (the talk box's tube) — one human-machine goal, half electronic, half flesh.

Also Pink Floyd's “Pigs” (Gilmour, 1979) · Bon Jovi's “Livin' on a Prayer” · Foo Fighters (Dave Grohl); ⭐ Daft Punk's 「robot」 act is often played on a talk box — eyes and ears both can be deceived (against the vocoder entry); ⭐ the 「Ghetto Talkbox」 — a homemade version, usually crude: tube + horn driver, anyone can build one (the folk democracy of effects).
