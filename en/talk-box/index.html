The talk box

The guitar borrows your mouth as its filter — the most bodily effect of the electronic age

1 min read146 characters条目ElectronicUpdated 2026-10-20

Christmas 1973: Bob Heil gave Peter Frampton a hand-built fibreglass box. Two years later Frampton stood on stage with a clear tube in his mouth, played a note, and the note came out of his mouth sounding like a voice saying Oh.

A tube that puts the sound in your mouth

Inside the pedal sits a horn driver with its horn removed. The sound passes through an airtight joint into a plastic tube whose end is taped at the microphone and held to the lips. The shape of the mouth changes the harmonic content, exactly as it changes the harmonics of the vocal folds in speech. What the microphone receives is an instrument reshaped by a mouth. The player never makes a sound; he only filters. The guitar borrows your mouth.

Three claimants and one puppet

In 1944 Alvino Rey had a puppet called Stringy open its mouth while a man offstage blew the tube. In 1964 Pete Drake's steel guitar spoke on record. In 1969 Kustom's The Bag became the first mass-produced unit: a 30-watt driver in a leather bag. In 1971 Bob Heil built a 250-watt version for Joe Walsh's tour. Doug Forbes has said the idea had earlier been patented as an artificial larynx, for people who had lost their voice. Instrument history borrowed a tube from medicine.

A Christmas present

Frampton is said to have shut himself away for two weeks to learn it. The difficulty is all in the mouth: forming words around a tube is the mouth's extreme sport. After Frampton Comes Alive! in 1975 he became more or less synonymous with the device, which some called a framptone. In 1988 Heil sold the manufacturing rights to Dunlop, which still builds it to the same specification. The company moved on; the specification stayed.

Two routes to a machine voice

Kraftwerk's machine voice ran on electricity, through a vocoder's filter banks. Roger Troutman of Zapp ran his on a mouth, through the tube. After More Bounce to the Ounce that speaking sound travelled into G-funk and on to T-Pain. A plastic tube and a horn driver: anyone can put one together.

When a voice that is not quite a voice comes out of a speaker, the first question is where it came from: a circuit, or a mouth.

  • 主线：滤波发生在嘴里（本页最该记住的一条） —— 「talk box 是一种效果器，允许音乐家通过改变嘴的形状来修改乐器的声音」；「嘴的形状改变谐波含量，其方式与说话时影响声带产生的谐波含量相同」；「这种 shaping 过的声音从演奏者口中出来，进入麦克风时，听到的是乐器/人声的混合体」； 对照 vocoder 条——滤波的位置是两者的分水岭：vocoder 的滤波发生在滤波器组（电子）里，talk box 的滤波发生在口腔（身体）里。talk box 是电子时代最「身体」的效果器
  • 发明争议三方（其他条目「名与实」谱系又一条） —— 「Pete Drake，纳什维尔的 pedal steel 乐手，在 1964 年专辑 Forever 上使用 talk box，后来被称为他的『会说话的钢吉他』（8 英寸纸盆喇叭接一根管）」；「Kustom Electronics 的『The Bag』是第一台量产的 talk box（1969，30W driver，挎在肩上的装饰包像皮水囊——Iron Butterfly/Steppenwolf/Steppenwolf）——Doug Forbes 声称设计，并说同一概念曾被专利为人工喉」；「Bob Heil 声称发明了 talk box——1971 年为 Joe Walsh 的 Barnstorm 巡演开发（250W JBL driver + hi-pass filter）」； 更早的幽灵：1944 年 Alvino Rey 用早期版本录制《St. Louis Blues》——木偶『Stringy』张嘴，人躲在旁边吹管——talk box 的祖先是木偶戏 + 医疗器械（人工喉）
  • Frampton 神话与 Heil 的传承 —— 「1973 年 Heil 把他的 talk box 当圣诞礼物送给 Peter Frampton（100W 手工玻璃纤维版）」；「Frampton 在专辑 Frampton Comes Alive! 上大量使用——由于专辑成功、特别是单曲 Show Me the Way 与 Do You Feel Like We Do，Frampton 几乎成了 talk box 的同义词——它甚至被叫作 framptone」；「1988 年 Heil 把制造权卖给 Dunlop Manufacturing——后者按完全相同的规格生产至今」； 对照「乐器比公司活得久」谱系：Heil 卖了规格，规格活了
  • funk 支线：Zapp 与 Roger Troutman —— 「Roger Troutman，funk 乐队 Zapp 的主唱，以使用 talk box 闻名——《More Bounce to the Ounce》」； 西海岸 G-funk 的 talk box 血统（T-Pain · Snoop Dogg）——funk 的「机器嗓子」与 Kraftwerk 的「机器嗓子」走的是两条路：一个用嘴，一个用电
  • 用户谱系（乐器名人堂） —— Joe Walsh《Rocky Mountain Way》（1973）· Jeff Beck《Blow By Blow》· Pink Floyd《Pigs》（1979）· Bon Jovi《Livin' on a Prayer》· Daft Punk（「机器人」形象常由 talk box 演出——眼见耳听都未必为实，对照 vocoder 条）· Foo Fighters（Dave Grohl）
