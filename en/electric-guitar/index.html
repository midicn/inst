How the electric guitar makes sound

The string disturbs a magnetic field and a current grows in the coil — the soundbox retires, the pickup clocks in

Related

Over a hundred instruments are done in this section, and nearly all of them make sound by something vibrating into air.

The electric guitar's strings vibrate too — but it never lets the air hear them.

「电吉他与传统吉他不同，不是以箱体的振动发声，而是运用了电磁学原理，它的琴身是实心的木头，而非空的音箱。」

⭐⭐ This page is the sound principle of the electric guitar. One line: the string disturbs a magnetic field, and a current grows in the coil.

⭐⭐ The main line: the string disturbs a field, the coil grows a current

Acoustic guitar: string vibrates → soundbox resonates → the air hears. Electric guitar: string vibrates → the field is disturbed → a current grows in the coil → amp → the air hears.

「当被磁化的吉他弦振动时，引起拾音器上线圈磁通量的变化，从而在线圈中产生不同频率的电流——当这些电流通过电子音箱还原时，就成了电吉他的声音。」

⭐⭐ Inside the pickup: 「内部包含永磁体与数千匝铜线绕制的线圈」. ⭐ A single-coil carries 「四或六块永磁体，绕 7000 至 10 000 匝 0.06 毫米细铜线」.

⭐⭐ And a lovely reversal of direction:

「导线在磁场中移动会产生电流——自行车发电机就是这个原理。拾音器也用这个原理，区别在于：不是导线在磁场里动，而是磁场在导线周围动，线圈保持固定」.

⭐⭐ The string's vibration keeps disturbing the field → the coil grows an alternating current proportional to it. The string is not "sounding" — it is "signalling".

⭐⭐ Why the body is solid: feedback forced the design

The earliest electric guitar was hollow + pickup. It had one fatal flaw:

「早期『空心电吉他』……有着很强的 feed-back 回授效应—— 使用时常会产生不需要的声音」.

⭐⭐ Les Paul's fix was brutal and brilliant（1941）:

「他将一把 epiphone 的 f 孔空心吉他切成两半，再将一块实心木块贴在中间，然后将两个拾音器固定在这块实心木块上，做成了有名的 log 吉他—— 他发现回授的问题再也没有发生了」.

⭐⭐ The solid body's motive was not tone — it was killing feedback— (and the by-product: a solid body 「抑制高增益放大时的反馈啸叫」 and 「显著延长琴弦振动的持续时间」 — sustain got longer.)

⭐ Commerce followed: 1941 log（prototype）→ 1948 Fender Broadcaster（first sold, soon renamed Telecaster）→ 1952 Les Paul solid → 1954 Stratocaster. ⭐ 1951 Precision Bass — the first electric bass: 「电贝斯让传统低音提琴在几个品类里几乎绝迹」.

⭐⭐ The humbucker: a part whose name is its mission

Single-coils have a birth defect — they are also little radios:

「所有磁性拾音器都会拾取环境中通常不想要的电磁干扰（EMI）——市电 hum， 50 或 60 赫兹；这种哼声在单线圈拾音器上特别强」.

⭐⭐ The double-coil's fix is beautiful engineering:

「两组相同的线圈反相串联，磁铁极性相反——干扰哼声两组线圈都收到，结果一组抵消另一组的哼声（buck the hum，名字由此而来）；为防止弦感应的信号也被抵消，两组磁铁方向相反 → 两路电流相加，信号强度是单线圈的两倍」.

⭐⭐ But there is a tonal price: 「双线圈听起来更暗、更低、更中庸——两个磁极间的距离使弦感应出两个略不同的信号，某些高频相互抵消」.

⭐⭐ A part whose name is its mission（humbucker = buck the hum）, and the「fatter」tone it threw in for free became rock's bedrock.

⭐⭐ Flaw into signature: feedback became the lead sound

Feedback was the hollow body's flaw. Then:

「Ted Nugent 早期所使用的 birdland 即此种（空心）吉他， 但 Ted Nugent 能控制此种回授而成为他的演奏技巧之一」; in the late 60s, 「吉他手开始故意制造回授」.

⭐⭐ Same family as the matouqin moving its bow outside the strings: turning the 「unwanted」 into the 「signature」. What separates an artist from a craftsman is often whether the flaw can be played.

⭐⭐ The pedal chain extended the freedom: 「60 年代末，吉他手把独立前级的增益调大，产生 fuzz 音色」; 「哇音踏板让演奏者用脚调整音色」; stompbox family:「立体声合唱 · fuzz · 哇音 · 镶边 · 压缩/延音 · 延迟 · 混响 · 相位偏移」— ⭐ the sound can be rewritten before it is even amplified.

⭐⭐ The digital era: even the pickup need not be magnetic

「2002 年 Gibson 宣布第一把数字吉他——内部完成模数转换，可用标准以太网线直连电脑」; 「2003 年 Line 6 Variax 用压电拾音器取代电磁拾音器， 内置电脑可建模多种乐器的声音」.

⭐⭐ From "string disturbs field" to "string presses a piezo" — seventy years from electromagnetic to piezo to digital.

⭐ The key point⭐⭐ the string disturbs a field, the coil grows a current — 「不是以箱体的振动发声，而是运用了电磁学原理」; the soundbox retires, the pickup clocks in
⭐⭐ The pickup⭐ permanent magnets + thousands of turns of fine wire（single-coil: 7,000–10,000 turns of 0.06 mm）; ⭐⭐ the reversal: not the wire moving in a field, but the field moving around the wire（a bicycle dynamo used the other way round）
⭐⭐ The humbucker⭐⭐ two coils, out-of-phase, opposite magnet poles → hum cancels, string signal adds（twice the strength）— the name is the mission（buck the hum）; ⭐ tonal price: darker, bassier, some highs cancel
⭐⭐ The solid body⭐⭐ Les Paul's log（1941）: cut a hollow guitar in half, glue a solid block between → feedback gone — the motive was not tone, it was killing feedback; by-product: longer sustain
⭐⭐ Flaw → signature⭐ Ted Nugent controlled feedback into technique; late-60s 「故意制造回授」— ⭐⭐ unwanted into signature（matouqin's bow-outside family）
⭐ Pedalsfuzz · wah-wah（with the foot）· chorus · flanger · compressor · delay · reverb · phaser — ⭐ rewritten before amplification
⭐ Digital era2002 Gibson digital guitar（Ethernet direct）· 2003 Variax（piezo pickups + onboard modelling）
⭐ Bass1951 Fender Precision Bass — made the double bass nearly obsolete in several genres
HSChordophone（plucked）· via electromagnetic pickup（filed under the electronic section）

The acoustic guitar's sound is a conspiracy of wood and air; the electric guitar's is a conspiracy of string and magnet.

⭐⭐ The wood only holds the string up — the act of sounding has moved from the body into the coil.
