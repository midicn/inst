The TR-909

Two media in one box — the office after hours recorded techno's heartbeat

⭐⭐ The main line: two media in one box

「909 是第一台使用采样的 Roland 鼓机——crash、ride 与 hi-hat 用采样；其它声音（kick、snare、toms、clap）用模拟合成」; 「据一位 Roland 代表说，工程师们觉得采样有一些缺点，因此选择了采样与模拟声音的组合」.

⭐⭐ Against the 808（pure analog, forced）and the 909（hybrid, chosen）: digital cymbals riding an analog kick — hybrid stopped being a compromise and became a genre. （techno's heartbeat is analog; techno's sparkle is 6-bit — neither medium could be spared.）

⭐⭐ Hoshiai's sampling story: the office after hours was the first sampling booth

「Hoshiai 录自己的鼓套件做镲片——用一对不匹配的 Paiste 与 Zildjian hi-hat 镲做 hi-hat，Paiste crash 做 crash 与 ride」; ⭐⭐ 「镲片是在 Roland 办公室录的——在员工下班之后——为了自然混响」; 「他以 6-bit 采样，在运行 CP/M-80 操作系统的电脑上编辑波形—— 没有加 EQ 也没有压缩」.

⭐⭐ Against the Fairlight's light pen（sampling by drawing）— the 909's sampling was the primitive school: played by himself, recorded after hours, untouched. ⭐⭐ 《打一锤来喊一声》: strike once, record once — that is how techno's heartbeat was captured. 「Mismatched cymbals」+「after-hours reverb」+「no EQ」 — the sparkle of techno is the sum of three compromises.

⭐⭐ Commercial failure（one year）

「909 是商业失败——用户更喜欢 LinnDrum 等竞品更真实的声音」; 「Roland 一年后停产，共造 10,000 台」.

⭐⭐ After the 808's name caught fire, the 909 was born into an era that trusted only sampling — its analog kick became the evidence of 「unrealistic」. ⭐⭐ And that very kick later became techno's heartbeat — the verdict of 「unrealistic」 was burned by the dancefloor.

⭐⭐ Detroit/Chicago's second-hand kids（the third mirror）

「1980 年代末，909 被芝加哥与底特律的制作人推红—— Derrick May · Frankie Knuckles · Jeff Mills——买了二手机」.

⭐⭐ 808 → 303 → 909: Roland's 「triple failure」 was picked up entirely by the same crowd of second-hand kids — the rhythmic foundations of techno, house and acid are all Roland's failures. ⭐⭐ Against the TB-303/808 pages: this is not coincidence but an ecology of 「cheap creation」 — the bankrupt company's warehouse was the avant-garde's instrument shop.

⭐⭐ The unintended use as MIDI hub（Roland did not anticipate it）

「作为第一台带 MIDI 的 Roland 鼓机，制作人用 909 作为 hub 来同步与音序其它机器—— Roland 没有预想过这种用法」; ⭐ 「因此 Roland 公司也被归功于 MIDI 技术的发明」.

⭐⭐ Against the midi-controller page: the first large-scale 「hub」 use of MIDI happened on a drum machine — the drum machine was not just 「the thing that plays drums」; it was the command desk of the 1983–85 studio. （⭐ Against eurorack's 「anything feeds anything」: the 909's hub was that idea in commercial form.）

⭐⭐ Sound: punchy versus booming

「808 以 booming 著称，909 听起来 aggressive and punchy」; 「clap 与 snare 经同一噪声源生成——同时演奏时产生 phasing effect」; 「可调的 shuffle/swing timing 给电子 pattern 加了人的感觉—— house 与 techno groove 的基础」.

⭐⭐ 「Shuffle」 was the first drum machine to turn 「human timing」 into a knob — against the sequencer page's shuffle（Korg SQ）— machine precision + a little 「inaccuracy」 = groove.

⭐⭐ 「The most sampled drum sound in history」

「909 kick arguably 是音乐史上被采样最多的鼓声」; 「First known commercial use：Skinny Puppy 的专辑 Remission（发行于 909 上市数月后）」; 「Björk 用它造 Hunter（1997）里的 militaristic 打击」.

⭐⭐ A failed machine produced the most-sampled single 「drum hit」 — its 「death」（one-year production）was the beginning of its endless replication.
