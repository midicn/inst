The MPC series

Sixteen pads and the birth of finger drumming — the clap-game players built hip-hop's skeleton

⭐⭐ This page is the MPC series — fourth of the drum-machine family. One line: the birth of finger drumming.

⭐⭐ The main line: the birth of finger drumming

「史上第一次，16 个力度感应橡胶垫的矩阵来触发采样（12-bit/40kHz 最多 13 秒）」.

⭐⭐ 「它 introduces 了『MPC workflow』——后来成为现代 beat 制作的标准」—

⭐⭐ Before the MPC, making beats was 「programming」（step-sequencing: pressing grids）; after the MPC, making beats was 「slapping」（hitting pads with the palm）.

⭐⭐ Against the site's control history: the keyboard gave dynamics to the fingertip · the Martenot gave them to the ribbon · the MPC gave them to the whole palm — the hands of the clap-game built hip-hop's skeleton.

⭐⭐ Two giants co-authoring（Linn × Cockerell）

「Akai Professional 聘请 Roger Linn——LinnDrum 鼓机的创造者——来设计 MPC」; 「Linn 与 David Cockerell（EMS 的联合创始人）一起设计了第一代 MPC，后者负责硬件」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's co-authorship genealogy: Moog×Deutsch · Smith×Kakehashi · Linn×Smith（Tempest）— ⭐⭐ the MPC60 = father of sampled drums × elder of EMS synthesizers. （EMS — the British company from the synth history — its elder's hand is inside the MPC.）

⭐⭐ 12-bit grit: the twin stars with the SP-1200

「12-bit 40kHz 采样，相比后来的 16-bit 机型有独特的 gritty and punchy character」.

⭐⭐ Against the samplers page's SP-1200（12-bit grit defined hip-hop）— 「SP-1200 与 MPC60 在 80 年代末 90 年代初瓜分了 hip-hop 制作」— two 12-bit characters, two genre skeletons: the SP-1200's grit versus the MPC's punch — the twin stars of hip-hop production.

⭐⭐ MPC swing: the legendary quantization

「音序器支持紧的 timing 分辨率与 swing quantization—— 后来被称为传奇的『MPC swing』」.

⭐⭐ Against the LinnDrum page — the same inventor's second generation: Linn invented the quantize+swing formula on the LinnDrum, and forged it into a brand on the MPC（「MPC swing」 became a proper noun）.

⭐⭐ Standalone: no computer attached

「Standalone Design：不像当时许多采样器，MPC60 是 all-in-one 制作设备—— 它不需要额外硬件或电脑」; 「内置音序器可处理 60,000 个 MIDI 事件 · 每序列 99 轨 · 99 patterns · 99 sequences」.

⭐⭐ Against the workstation page: the workstation housed the 「studio」; the MPC housed the 「entire beat production line」— it is not an arranging machine; it is a beat machine: 「pattern-based · loop-focused · hands-on · minimal mouse」.

⭐⭐ Lineage（Linn's two machines and the post-Linn era）

「MPC60（1988）→ MKII（1991）→ MPC3000（1994 · 16-bit/44.1kHz stereo · 32 voices · the last Linn design）」→「MPC2000（1997）· 2000XL（1999）→ 4000（2002 · 24-bit）· 1000（2004）· 500（2006 · battery）· 2500（2007）」→ 「MPC Live/X（2017 · touchscreen standalone）· One · Key 61（2020s）」.

⭐⭐ 「Linn 的合作在 90 年代末结束」 — companies change designers; the philosophy on the pads does not. （Against the site: Fairlight's Vogel turned to film · Linn to Akai then LinnStrument — companies die, people walk, the pads remain.）

⭐⭐ The player lineage: hip-hop's hall of fame

「DJ Premier · Pete Rock · Marley Marl（the MPC60 era）」; 「J Dilla 在早期 MPC60 大量工作后转 MPC3000」; 「Kanye West · MF DOOM · Pete Rock · RJD2 · Marco Polo（2000XL）」; 「Dr. Dre（3000）」.

⭐⭐ Against the LinnDrum's Prince — every flagship has a hall-of-fame list — the MPC's list is the hall of fame of hip-hop production itself.

⭐⭐ J Dilla: the extreme of playing the MPC as an instrument

「在最受尊敬的 beatmaker 之中，J Dilla 也在早期 MPC60 上大量工作，后转 MPC3000」.

⭐⭐ Dilla's 「off-grid」 playing（deliberately un-quantized） pushed the MPC swing's 「machine humanised」 to its opposite — he did not fix the beat; he let it breathe. ⭐⭐ Against the 909's shuffle knob: Dilla made with his fingers the sway no knob could — the highest proof of 「finger drumming」: the pads are not buttons; they are keys.

⭐⭐ From Renaissance to standalone（2010s）

「DAW 崛起后，standalone 硬件逐渐被软件掩盖——五年间隔后， MPC Renaissance（2012）标记了重大转向：复古垫硬件 + 专用 MPC 软件」; 「MPC Live 与 MPC X（2017）：standalone 设备把经典机器的 hands-on 手感与现代触屏控制合并——免去电脑」.

⭐⭐ Against the software-synth page: software 「killed」 hardware once; thirty years later hardware returned with touchscreens — the MPC circle: hardware → software → touchscreen hardware. （What died was the form; what did not die were those sixteen pads.）
