The vocoder

From wartime cipher to the robot's singing voice — one invention, two destinies

⭐⭐ The main line: one invention, two destinies

「声码器由 Bell Labs 的 Homer Dudley 于 1938 年发明，作为合成人类语音的手段；这项工作发展为通道声码器，用于电信的语音编码以在传输中节省带宽」.

⭐⭐ One circuit, two names in two worlds:

⭐ Telecom world⭐ Music world
Namevoice encoder（secure comms）vocoder effect（robot voice）
Goalbandwidth · encryptiondehumanisation · futurism
MilestonesSIGSALY（1943）→ LPC-10（2.4kbps）Moog/Carlos → Kraftwerk → Daft Punk

⭐⭐ Against the site: the Telharmonium's technology 「died」 of its era; the vocoder's 「lives」 in two eras — because it held the needs of two worlds at once.

⭐⭐ The Voder（1939）: speech synthesis played by a human

「为了演示其解码器的语音合成能力，Voder（Voice Operation Demonstrator）在 1939–1940 年纽约世博会的 AT&T 馆向公众推出」; 「Voder 由一个电子振荡器（有调音源）与噪声发生器（嘶声）组成、 10 带共鸣滤波器（可变增益放大器）做声道、以及手动控制器（压力感应键控滤波 + 踏板控音高）」.

⭐⭐ 「这是一台操作复杂的机器，但熟练的操作员能产生可辨认的语音」—

⭐⭐ The Voder/Vocoder division: the Voder was 「speech synthesis played by a human」（hand-controlled filters）; the Vocoder was 「automatic speech encoding」（machine analysis）— two names, two degrees of automation.

⭐⭐ The principle: discard the pitch, keep the shape

「分析：输入信号经多带滤波器，每带输出由包络跟随器测量；合成：解码器把这些作为控制信号施加到输出滤波通道的相应放大器」; ⭐⭐ 「原始语音信号的瞬时频率信息被丢弃了——对加密而言这并不重要」— ⭐⭐ 「正是这个 dehumanizing（去人性化）的方面，使它在流行音乐中成为有用的特殊声音效果」.

⭐⭐ The sharpest 「engineering-art swap」 on the site: to the encryption engineer, 「discarding pitch」 was the price of compression; to the musician, 「discarding pitch」 is why the machine sings — the insight that sound's 「shape」 can be separated from its 「pitch」 gave birth to both the secure phone and the robot's singing voice.

⭐⭐ The musical turn

「数十年后，Robert Moog 与 Wendy Carlos 造了一台耦合到 Moog 模块化合成器的声码器，把如今标志性的机器人嗓音引入音乐」; 「Carlos 用它为 Stanley Kubrick 的电影 A Clockwork Orange 人声化贝多芬第九交响曲的第四乐章」; 「德国电子先驱 Kraftwerk 在 1974 年专辑 Autobahn 上使用声码器，随后影响了一整代电子音乐家」.

⭐⭐ Against the moog-modular page's Switched-On Bach（Carlos）— Carlos's two 「classical × electronic」 crossings: playing Bach on a synthesizer（1968）, singing Beethoven through a vocoder（A Clockwork Orange）— each rewrote what electronic instruments could do.

⭐⭐ Three ways a machine sings: vocoder / Auto-Tune / talkbox

⭐ VocoderAuto-Tune⭐⭐ Talkbox
Principlespectral transplant（two inputs）pitch correction（one input）acoustic filtering（physical）
Pitchuntouchedchangedchanged（by lips）
Where filteredelectronic filter bankssoftwarethe performer's mouth
LandmarksKraftwerk · Daft PunkCher《Believe》· T-PainFrampton · Zapp

⭐⭐ The talkbox's 「filtering happens inside the performer's mouth」 — against the site's 「body as instrument」 lineage（jaw harp/throat carvings/kalimba resonators）— the talkbox is the most 「bodily」 effect of the electronic age.

⭐⭐ The telecom continuation: parameters, not waveforms

「早期数字电话系统使用脉冲编码调制，需要 64 kbps 传输一路语音—— 那是很大的带宽」; 「基于线性预测编码（LPC）的声码器通过建模声道、只传输重建语音所需的参数而非完整音频波形，大幅降低带宽——美国政府标准 LPC-10 只用 2.4 kbps」.

⭐⭐ 「只传参数不传波形」 — the vocoder's philosophy became the foundation of digital communication: against the Prophet-5's patch memory（「remember parameters, not audio」）— 「parameterisation」 is the fundamental idea of digital audio, and the vocoder was its first massive application.

⭐⭐ Cher 1998 and the software version of flaw-into-signature

「重度应用把人声锁到最近的半音太快，把歌手的音高规训到不自然的机器完美—— 常被误认为声码器效果，合成的 Auto-Tune 摆音由 Cher 的 1998 年金曲 Believe 推广，此后被 Kanye West · T-Pain · Will.i.am 使用（可以说过度使用）」.

⭐⭐ Against the TB-303（flaw into signature） — Auto-Tune's 「over-correction」 is also a flaw become signature — but a 「software flaw」, not a 「hardware flaw」. Twice the 「wrong use」 was adopted by music: the 303 took 7 years, Believe took 1.

The vocoder's story is 「one circuit, two reincarnations」: first born as the secure phone, reborn as the robot's singing voice. ⭐⭐ Its philosophy — 「parameters, not waveforms」 — still governs digital audio: you think you are listening to sound; often you are listening to parameters.
