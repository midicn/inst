The electric violin and cello

The disappearing body — the resonance box dismantled piece by piece, the sound handed to electricity

2 min readElectronicUpdated 2026-10-20

A horn on a violin

In 1899 a London maker, Johannes Matthias Augustus Stroh, patented a violin whose body had been reshaped around a metal horn. The bridge fed a diaphragm, the diaphragm drove the bell, and the sound left forward instead of in every direction.

Stroh trained as a watchmaker in Frankfurt, then moved to London and took up electrical engineering. He did not build it for concert halls. Acoustic recording gathered sound through a large horn, an ensemble crowded in front of it, and much of a violin's output never arrived. A horn aimed the sound into the recording trumpet; a carved body could not.

In 1911 Barnes & Mullins sold one for nine guineas against two for a factory violin. In Buenos Aires Julio de Caro played tango on it, and people called it the cornet violin.

It had to be kept

Early acoustic recording captured a band of roughly 100 to 2,500 Hz, and most of a violin's overtones fell outside it. What shimmered in a hall came off the disc thin.

So the first motive was preservation, not volume. When record companies switched to electric microphones in the later 1920s, the Stroh violin lost its job; it had lasted under thirty years. The second motive was the stadium, where only pickups and speakers could carry. Studios and arenas, not halls, are where this family began.

From the f-hole to a stick

R. F. Starzl fixed a pickup at the f-hole in 1927. Victor Pfeil built a skeleton violin in 1928 with the pickup under the bridge. In 1930 L'Illustration showed Cecilia Hansen playing an instrument with no resonating body, cabled to an amplifier.

Beauchamp's 1935 Rickenbacker was a bakelite stick; National's 1936 Vio-Electric was played by Stuff Smith. Once the body no longer had to hold air, no single shape survived.

In June 1958 Leo Fender filed for a patent and showed the violin that October in Nashville: hollow maple body, his own pickup, a rocking bridge moving an armature across a single coil, nearer a spark plug than a microphone, with the gap set to thousandths of an inch. Almost nobody has seen one. In 2005 a leading specialist said he had seen two in forty years; no auction house had handled one.

After the body

Pfeil imagined an electric cello; Stroh extended the design to viola, cello and bass as Stroviols. From the 1990s silent violins sent everything to headphones, MIDI instruments turned bow speed into data, and five- and seven-string models pushed the range outward. No standard form survives because no piece of wood has to stay.

Listen for what is missing rather than for resemblance: the wooden colour is gone, and a pickup reads the string directly.

Sources

  • 主线：琴身的消失（本页最该记住的一条） —— 电小提琴的历史是「共鸣箱被一块块拆掉」的历史：木琴身 → 金属膜 + 喇叭（Stroh 1899）→ 拾音器贴在木琴身上（Starzl 1927 · Stuff Smith）→ 骨架琴 / 无琴身棍（Pfeil 1928 · Makhonine 1930 · Rickenbacker 1935）→ 空心身 + 独特拾音（Fender 1958）→ 压电与 MIDI → 静音； 每一次拆掉一块木头，「提琴」的定义就宽一分——到最后，琴可以只是一根棍、一块板、甚至一个可以戴在身上的框架。琴身的消失不是乐器的死亡，是乐器从「一件东西」变成「一个界面」
  • 动机：录不下的声音 —— 「从 1877 年声学时代黎明开始，录音会议要求乐手把声音灌进一只喇叭——系统只捕捉频谱的窄窄一条，大约 100–2,500Hz——小提琴的大量泛音 richness 从未到达沟槽」；「大厅里 shimmer 的，到唱片上变得又薄又幽灵」； 电小提琴不是为了让提琴更响，是为了让提琴「留得下」——先是录音，后是球场与巡演（「在 stadium 里听原声乐器已无可能」）——一件乐器的电声化，动机往往不在音乐厅，在录音棚和体育场
  • Stroh 1899：钟表匠 + 喇叭（对照 Kakehashi） —— 「Johannes Matthias Augustus Stroh 在 Frankfurt 受钟表匠训练，后定居伦敦转攻电气工程；1899 年他专利了一只机械放大提琴，用膜片 + 金属号筒」；「声音被集中进录音 horn 的方式，是雕花的木身体做不到的」；「1911 年伦敦 Barnes & Mullins 卖九畿尼——当时一把像样的工厂琴两畿尼」；「1920 年代布宜诺斯艾利斯的 Julio de Caro 用它，当地人叫它 violín-corneta（短号提琴）」；「1920 年代后半唱片公司转向电麦克风录音后，Stroh 提琴变得稀少」； 一位钟表匠造了「给录音喇叭的提琴」（对照 Roland 的钟表出身 Kakehashi）——而它活了三十年，然后被自己开启的时代淘汰
  • 电小提琴的出生证明是一堆专利 —— 「Elisha Gray 1874 年实验『提琴的电气化』——在小提琴上装金属板接收并放大电振荡——但在他的实验里，提琴扮演的是放大器（ Musical Telegraph 是世界第一件电乐器）」；「R.F. Starzl 1927：拾音器贴在 f 孔——已知最早的 electric-acoustic 提琴之一」；「Victor Pfeil 1928：骨架琴，电磁拾音器藏在琴马正下方」；「Ivan Makhonine 1930：法国《L'Illustration》报道 Cecilia Hansen 拉一把没有共鸣体、用电缆接放大器的琴」；「Rickenbacker Electro Violin 1935：George Beauchamp 设计，电木（后铝/木）『棍子』——极简设计证明了传统乐器形态改变的可能性」；「National Vio-Electric 1936：John Dopyera 开发，爵士小提琴家『Stuff』Smith 演奏——拾音器由两枚小磁线圈组成」； 「多数关于电小提琴起源的信息现在只能在专利里找到」——一项技术的童年，常常只有法律文书作证
  • Fender 1958：一件几乎不存在的名器 —— 「Leo Fender 早在 1948 年就在想电小提琴——计划完成电弦乐全套（吉他、贝斯、曼陀林、提琴）」；「1958 年 6 月 2 日申请『electric violin and electromagnetic pickup therefor』；1958 年 10 月纳什维尔 WSM 展首发（sunburst 漆 · 空心枫木身 · bolt-on 琴颈 · Kluson 齿轮弦轴）」；「拾音器是 Fender 自己的发明：琴马 rocking → 金属 pad（armature）振动 → 扰动单线圈拾音器的磁场——像汽车引擎的火星塞；调节间隙 profoundly 影响输出（『大于 1/32 英寸，最好约 1/64 英寸』）」；「2005 年时，一位世界领先的 vintage 电乐器专家 40 年里只见过两把；苏富比/佳士得/邦瀚斯从未上拍过一把」； 名门 + 名器 + 几乎无人见过——电小提琴史上的「幽灵名器」（对照「失败品仓库」谱系：这一件不是失败，是被忘记）
  • 电大提琴与现代 —— 「Pfeil 也构想了几件电弦乐器，包括一把 cello，用磁拾音」；「Stroh 把设计扩展到 viola、cello 甚至 double bass，统称 Stroviols」； 现代对照：「Yamaha Silent Violin 与 1930 年 Makhonine 的无琴身提琴的相似」——六十年，同一个答案被两次给出：把琴身拿掉，声音就有了自由；MIDI 小提琴（Zeta 等）把弓速/压力变成 MIDI；五弦、六弦、七弦扩展音域—— 电提琴的尽头是「什么都行」：没有标准形制，因为琴身已经不在
  • 对照「去身体化」谱系（auto-tune 条） —— Auto-Tune 去掉了歌者的身体（音高被直接挪位），电小提琴去掉了琴的身体（共鸣被交给拾音器与音箱）—— 两条去身体化的路线在同一时代完成：一个让「唱」不再需要嗓子，一个让「提琴」不再需要琴身。而 talk box 条的口腔滤波提醒我们：也有反方向的——把身体接回去
  • 用户谱系 —— Jean-Luc Ponty（fusion 小提琴）· Mahavishnu Orchestra 的 Jerry Goodman · Tom Waits · They Might Be Giants（《I Can Hear You》——在爱迪生实验室的蜡筒上录，用 Stroh 提琴）· Shakira 巡演（Una Palliser）
