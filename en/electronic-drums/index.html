⭐⭐ The main line: the mesh on a trampoline

「为了得到自然的手感，我们同意用类似薄膜或皮肤的材料做鼓垫面。一天，机械设计师 Mr. Yoshino 走访一家 DIY 木工园艺超市—— 偶然发现一个小蹦床，它的弹跳面用的是网状材料。他来了灵感：Roland 可以用网面做鼓垫」.

⭐⭐ 「日本找不到好的网面代工——幸运的是 Mr. Kakehashi（Roland 创始人）把我们介绍给 Mr. Remo Belli（Remo 创始人）。我们请他为我们的网状鼓皮代工，他同意了」.

⭐⭐ 「我们发现某种网、双层、织纹呈 45 度角最好——我们申请了专利」.

⭐⭐ The folk mirror: 《蹦鼓子》 — the drum that bounced out of a trampoline. The site's unplanned-invention genealogy gains another line: the theremin from an alarm · the xun from a hunting tool · the TB-303 from failure · the mesh head from a DIY-store trampoline.

⭐⭐ The core brief: acoustic feel without the acoustic noise

「橡胶垫更像键盘的『synth』手感——我们想帮鼓手获得原声手感。挑战是：得到原声手感而没有原声噪音」.

⭐⭐ Two accidents in relay:

DiscoveryResult
①a trampoline in a DIY store⭐ the mesh head（two plies · 45-degree weave · patented）
⭐ ②playing the prototype upside-down「壳底浇的橡胶手感好又安静」→ asked Remo to pour rubber on the top hoop too → the whole performance stays quiet

⭐⭐ 「整个演奏保持安静——这是电鼓的关键好处」— against the digital-piano page（the Clavinova's no-maintenance/no-tuning/no-space）— the electronic drum's three gifts: silence · no tuning · unlimited sounds.

⭐⭐ Prehistory: Syndrum to Simmons

「第一款上市的电子鼓是 Syndrum——1/Twin/Quad 三个型号（1/2/4 个触发器）」; 「1978 年 Dave Simmons 创立 Simmons——六角形 pads」; 「其声音被 80 年代最大牌艺术家使用：A Flock of Seagulls · Duran Duran · Def Leppard · Prince · Phil Collins · Rush · Depeche Mode」.

⭐⭐ Against the site: Simmons' hexagons were the drum of the 「synth-look」 era — the shape itself was the declaration: not a kit of drums — a synthesizer standing on a rack.

⭐⭐ Octapad: the percussion MIDI keyboard

「Roland Octapad——1985 年发布——是第一个用于触发采样与任何 MIDI 设备的 MIDI 控制器」.

⭐⭐ Against the midi-controller page — the Octapad was the 「percussion MIDI keyboard」: drummers played 「other people's sound sources」 on drums for the first time — 8 pads（Octa）+ the power to trigger anything = the controllerisation of the percussionist.

⭐⭐ V-Drums: COSM modeling and the ecosystem

「1997 年 Roland 发布革命性的 V-Drums——TD-10：网状鼓皮垫 + COSM 建模（来自 V-Guitar 的技术）——实际演奏方面与声音引擎设计一起考虑」; 「2012 TD-30KV：SuperNATURAL 音源 + Behavior Modeling—— 以空前的真实度与细微差别重现原声鼓声」.

⭐⭐ The V-Drums chronology is a complete 「accessory ecosystem」 chain: 1998 mesh kick · 2001 independently sensed electronic cymbals（bow/edge apart）· 2004 floating V-hi-hat · 2007 home all-in-one · 2008 marching · 2011 drummer social app — ⭐⭐ Against the workstation page: an electronic kit is not one instrument; it is an ecosystem — the 「box that carries everything」 logic extended to drums.

⭐⭐ 「The electric guitar for drummers」

「V-Drums 将成为鼓手的电吉他——允许安静练习与无限的音色可能性。无需调音，一根 MIDI 线简化录音——革命化鼓组的理念」.

⭐⭐ Against the electric-guitar page — electrification rewrote the guitar's fate（from jazz accompaniment to rock protagonist）— the V-Drums vision did the same for drums: from 「the loudest member of the rhythm section」 to 「a complete instrument playable with headphones」.

⭐⭐ Roland: from boutique to 「the biggest drum company」

「近 40 年对电子打击乐创新的坚持，把 Roland 的地位抬高到成为世界上最大的鼓公司」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's company histories: Roland's failure warehouse rewrote music history （808/303/909）— and the electronic drum is the正面 achievement of this 「failure museum」: from Kakehashi's founding（1972）to 「the biggest drum company」 — the electronic drum is the company's coming of age.
