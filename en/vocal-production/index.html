How the voice works

The voice is blown into being, not struck by nerves — self-sustained oscillation

Related

⭐⭐ The main line: the voice is blown into being, not struck by nerves

「The vocal muscles set the initial conditions — such as the degree of adduction, tension, and length of the vocal folds — but do not directly drive the vibratory cycle」.

⭐⭐ The nervous system does not strike the note; it only sets the boundary conditions — the rest is left to the wind. (The voice entry covered the three-stage mechanism; this entry covers 「why it keeps sounding by itself」 — a self-sustained instrument.)

⭐⭐ Bernoulli is not enough — geometry completes it

PhaseGlottal geometryAerodynamic force
Openingconvergent (closed at bottom, open at top)pushes outward — assists opening
Closingdivergent (the reverse)presses inward — assists closing

⭐⭐ 「Bernoulli forces……Not well at all, researchers have found. Soon, oscillation would damp out」 — the early explanation could not sustain self-sustained oscillation; Titze 1980's revision completed the mechanism: ⭐⭐ 「the aerodynamic force on the folds is always slightly in phase with their motion — pushing them in whichever direction they are already moving」 — the wind always pushes with it.

⭐⭐ The mucosal wave is the geometry's source: 「a traveling surface wave……inferior-to-superior direction」; 「the body (thyroarytenoid muscle) is mechanically stiffer than the cover (vocal ligament and mucosa)」 — ⭐⭐ 「not a decorative feature of fold vibration but the tissue-mechanical mechanism that makes self-sustained MEAD oscillation possible」.

⭐⭐ The phonation threshold: 2–3 cmH₂O

⭐⭐ 「a minimum amount of lung pressure……roughly 2 to 3 centimeters of water pressure — a very small force」 — everything starts with one breath.

⭐⭐ 「If your vocal folds are dry, swollen, or stiff, that threshold rises — this is why your voice feels effortful when you're dehydrated」 — ⭐⭐ the physics of a hoarse voice is a raised threshold, not smaller lungs.

⭐⭐ Frequency is managed by an antagonistic pair: the cricothyroid elongates and tenses (raises pitch) · the thyroarytenoid shortens and relaxes (lowers it) — an oscillator's coarse and fine tuning. Male 85–180Hz · female 165–255Hz · children about 250Hz.

⭐⭐ The singer's formant: the gap that pierces an orchestra

「a concentration of acoustic energy around 2,500 to 3,500 Hz — illustrates how the entire vocal system, not just the vocal cords, shapes the final output」.

⭐⭐ The voice does not compete with the orchestra in its loudest region (the low frequencies); it opens a 2,500–3,500Hz formant band elsewhere and slips through the gap — ⭐⭐ tuned for penetration: training and evolution did half each.

⭐⭐ The timbre spectrum: two failure directions of the same machine — 「breathy (excessive airflow with incomplete glottal closure) → pressed (tight adduction with reduced airflow) → a balanced 『flow』 phonation」: one closes too loosely, the other lacks airflow — a 「good voice」 is the flow balance in between.

⭐ The first mathematical model that 「sounds by itself」

⭐⭐ The two-mass model (Ishizaka & Flanagan, 1972): 「each vocal fold represented as two coupled masses……the lower mass opens and closes slightly ahead of the upper」 — the first model that could compute self-sustained oscillation, hundreds of thousands of years after humans started singing.

⭐⭐ Against the site's oscillation sources: the Telharmonium's electricity · the Rhythmicon's computation · the voice's wind — three 「non-human」 oscillation sources, and wind is the oldest (against 《大风吹来竹叶飞》: wind scatters bamboo leaves; wind makes folds sound).

  • ⭐⭐ Bernoulli 不够，几何来凑（Titze 1980） —— 「Two decades of subsequent modeling and experiment revealed that the Bernoulli effect, while real, is not the primary closing mechanism in most phonatory conditions」；「According to these theories, Bernoulli forces cause the vocal folds to be sucked together……Not well at all, researchers have found. Soon, oscillation would damp out」；⭐⭐ Titze 1980（Journal of Speech and Hearing Research）的修正：主导机制是声门的收敛-发散几何——「During the opening phase, the folds move apart from the bottom upward, creating a convergent channel——narrower at the top than the bottom. During the closing phase……a divergent channel」；「In a convergent glottis the air pressure pushes outward——a net opening force; in a divergent glottis the pressure distribution reverses, producing a net inward force that assists closure」；⭐⭐ 「the aerodynamic force on the folds is always slightly in phase with their motion——pushing them in whichever direction they are already moving——which is precisely the condition for sustained energy transfer」——⭐⭐ 一台自持振荡的乐器：风永远顺着它推
  • ⭐⭐ 黏膜波（mucosal wave）：让几何成为可能的行波 —— 「a traveling surface wave that propagates along the medial surface of each vocal fold in the inferior-to-superior direction during each vibratory cycle」；「the body (thyroarytenoid muscle) is mechanically stiffer than the cover (vocal ligament and mucosa), and the stiffness discontinuity between the two layers allows a surface wave to propagate upward」；⭐⭐ 「The mucosal wave is therefore not a decorative feature of fold vibration but the tissue-mechanical mechanism that makes self-sustained MEAD oscillation possible」——一层松一层紧的分层结构，是自持振荡的硬件前提（病理学注脚：疤痕或 Reinke 水肿限制黏膜波 → 粗糙、费力的嗓音）
  • ⭐⭐ 起振门槛：2–3 cmH₂O —— 「There is a minimum amount of lung pressure required to get the vocal folds vibrating, called the phonation threshold pressure. For a healthy voice, this is roughly 2 to 3 centimeters of water pressure——a very small force」；「If your vocal folds are dry, swollen, or stiff, that threshold rises——this is why your voice feels effortful when you're dehydrated or have laryngitis」；⭐⭐ 万事开头只差一口气——而「嗓子哑了」的物理学是门槛升高，不是肺变小了
  • ⭐⭐ 频率与对抗肌 —— 成人男声 85–180Hz（说话）· 女声 165–255Hz · 儿童中位数约 250Hz（随身高与喉头增大而降）；「the cricothyroid muscle elongates and tenses the vocal folds by tilting the thyroid cartilage forward relative to the cricothyroid cartilage, thereby raising the fundamental frequency; the thyroarytenoid (vocalis) muscle shortens and relaxes the folds, lowering the pitch」；⭐⭐ 一对对抗肌 = 一台振荡器的粗调与微调（对照合成器：cricothyroid 是粗调旋钮，thyroarytenoid 是细调）
  • ⭐⭐ Singer's formant：穿透乐队的那道缝 —— 「The concept of the singer's formant——a concentration of acoustic energy around 2,500 to 3,500 Hz——illustrates how the entire vocal system, not just the vocal cords, shapes the final output」；⭐⭐ 人声不与乐队争最响处（低频区），另开一个 2.5–3.5kHz 的共振峰频段钻空档——「为穿透而调」的特征，训练与演化各做了一半
  • ⭐⭐ 声质谱与声区 —— 「Variations in these parameters can produce a wide range of vocal qualities, from breathy (excessive airflow with incomplete glottal closure) to pressed (tight adduction with reduced airflow) to a balanced or 『flow』 phonation that is often considered optimal」；⭐⭐ breathy 与 pressed 是同一台机器的两个故障方向：一个闭合不紧，一个气流不够——「好声音」是中间的流量平衡
  • ⭐⭐ flow-induced oscillation 的普适性 —— 「A steady stream of air passing by a wall or surface can cause that surface to vibrate——this can be seen in the way airplane wings vibrate in flight, and in the rattling of air ducts in heating systems」；⭐⭐ 吹过缝隙的风让万物唱歌——声带不是特例，是流体力学的一个实例（对照全站：Telharmonium 的电 · Rhythmicon 的算 · 声带的风——三种「非人」的振荡源，风是最古老的那个）
