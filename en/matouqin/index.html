Matouqin

The one exception in the whole bowed-string family — its bow does not sit between the strings

Related

SihuErhuZhuihuZhonghuRegisterTimbre

Across this section we have written nine bowed strings — the erhu, banhu, jinghu, gaohu, zhonghu, gehu, zhuihu, yehu and sihu. They share one property, and this page is the first to name it: their bow hair sits between the two strings.

「拉奏方法也与其它拉弦乐器不同，⭐⭐ 琴弓的弓毛不夹在里 · 外弦之间，而是在两弦外面擦奏」

⭐⭐ Main line 57: the one exception in the whole bowed-string family — the bow is outside the strings.

⭐⭐ One exception, worth a whole page

One sentence separates it from the others: in those nine the hair passes between the strings; in the matouqin it grazes outside them.

What it buys—⭐ because the bow is outside, it can sound two pitches at once

⭐ That last row is the real dividend of being an exception: with the hair outside, both strings can be grazed at once. The other instruments have to work for double stops — the sihu splits its bow hair in two, the erhu presses the hair against both strings. The matouqin needs no special technique at all: it can do it by nature.

⭐⭐ Skin on two faces — the second exception

「它最突出的特点是，琴箱的面 · 背两面都蒙皮膜，这与一般的拉弦类乐器有显著的不同。」

⭐⭐ Another exception. In all those other instruments the skin covers only the front opening (the back carries a sound hole). The matouqin is skinned on both faces.

The others⭐ Matouqin
Skinfront opening only⭐ both the front and the back

The consequence is audible: sealing a smaller box makes the sound thicker and darker — which is exactly where its 「浑厚 · 深沉」 comes from. Form decides tone; this is not a coincidence.

⭐⭐ "Calling it two strings is not quite accurate"

「⭐ 习惯上称它为两根弦是不确切的，因为每根弦都是由数十根至百余根组成的一撮弦……一般高音弦可用 90 根尼龙丝，低音弦可用 130 根，⭐ 过多则硬，过少则软，都会影响马头琴的音色。」

⭐⭐ It has only two strings, but each is a bundle of dozens to over a hundred filaments. Every other instrument here has a single string per note; the matouqin rings a sheaf of silk.

⭐ And the count matters: too many and it is hard, too few and it is soft — not a free parameter but one that must be tuned.

The strings themselves have moved through horsehair → nylon → metal; the reform 「用尼龙丝弦代替了马尾弦，将定弦提高了四度音程，扩大了音域，在保留原有柔和 · 深厚的音色的同时，增加了清晰 · 明亮 · 富有力度的特点」.

⭐⭐ Holding it: the little finger reaches under

Posture: seated, the box clamped between the legs, neck inclined to the left.

Left hand: the thumb steadies the neck; in the low position the index and middle fingers press with the roots of their nails and the ring and little fingers press with their tips — and here is the detail unique to this instrument:

⭐⭐「小指在演奏中非常重要，它常从外弦下面伸进去顶里弦」

In the high position all fingers use their tips. Right hand: the tiger's mouth grips the handle, index and middle on the stick, ring and little controlling the hair.

⭐⭐ And the crucial sentence comes last:

「⭐ 运弓中，弓毛和琴弦要保持直角状态。」

⭐⭐ Why? Because the hair is outside the strings — it rests beside them rather than being held by them, so the angle has to be managed by the player. In the other instruments the hair is trapped between the strings; here it is set against them from outside.

⭐⭐ Three reforms, two of them forced by the weather

RoundYearWhat changedWhy
(teacher)1962a 5-chi instrument shortened to 80 cm; cowhide replaced; a pitch post added「没有音准，也没有音量」
⭐ 1st1973snare-drum skin → python skin⭐⭐ A downpour. Performing in the Xilingol steppe, 「天空突降倾盆大雨，而牧民们仍然一动不动地坐在草地上欣赏他的独奏」 →「雨水打湿了马头琴的定音鼓面，声音开始嘶哑进而失声」. The practical reason: 「蟒皮不怕潮湿，它能跟交响乐团合作 10 分钟不跑弦；原来 5 分钟就跑弦」，so accompanists declined
⭐ 2nd1982python → paulownia wood face⚠️ The first attempt failed. He arrived with 20 yuan, spent 12 on the train ticket, and commissioned the box; 「指挥说变成了 18 元钱小提琴的声音了，不是马头琴了」. He went back: 「木面太厚了，得弄薄，薄了以后声音才能出来」 — that afternoon the sound came out
⭐ 3rd1996white pine sound boxin ensemble, tutti, concerto and with symphony orchestras the tone improved dramatically, 「极具穿透力」

⭐⭐ What the three have in common: none of them began with an idea about what would be better — each began when something broke. Rain spoiled the drum skin → python. Python slipped → wood. Wood was too thick → make it thinner. Every time, "first it broke, then we changed it."

The third reform was not the instrument but the technique: techniques from the violin, erhu, sihu and cello, plus 跳弓 · 击弓 · 碎弓 · 抖弓 · 连跳弓 · 砍弓 · 连砍弓, and a scientific sorting of the old tunings and playing methods.

「我 16 岁在中央音乐学院学了小提琴以后，发现马头琴是太落伍了。我也要改！」

⭐⭐ "The only instrument that has a head"

「马头琴是成吉思汗留下来的。它是唯一有脑袋的乐器，有了脑袋就有了五脏，有了五脏就有了灵魂。」

⭐⭐ Everything else in this section is a box, a neck, strings and a bow. Only the matouqin has one more thing at the top: a carved horse head — in two forms, a galloping horse and a standing one. It is not decoration; it is where the instrument's name and its entire origin story come from.

Origin legends (both given as legend only): a herdsman named Suhe took his dead white horse's leg bone as a post, its skull as the box, its tail hair as strings, and carved a head in the horse's image; and the version Qi Baoligao accepts, in which the old herder Ochir makes the whole instrument from a bay horse's bones, skin and tail.

⚠️ He also offers a third: 「我为了寻找马头琴的起源还去了故宫，在那里发现了2500 年前库莫奚部落的奚琴」, and that 「马头琴被带走后，意大利才做出了小提琴」. — ⭐ Place and date are recorded exactly as the source gives them; they are not asserted as history.

⭐⭐ Starting a matouqin orchestra

In 1986 he proposed a matouqin troupe, gathering skilled players from every arts organisation in Inner Mongolia; ⭐ 「费了九牛二虎之力，才将这些演奏员的弓法 · 节奏 · 指法统一起来」. The ensemble became the Qi Baoligao Yema Matouqin Troupe — the world's first matouqin orchestra. On 20 June 1989 the Chinese Matouqin Society was founded in Hohhot, with him as president.

⭐⭐ Note the difficulty in 「统一弓法 · 节奏 · 指法」: every player had a system handed down by their own teacher. Bringing two dozen people from their separate habits onto one page took 「九牛二虎之力」.
