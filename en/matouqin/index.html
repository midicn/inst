Matouqin

The one exception in the whole bowed-string family — its bow does not sit between the strings

3 min readChinese BowedUpdated 2026-10-03

Nine bowed instruments came before this one, and in all nine the bow hair runs between the two strings. The matouqin is the exception: the hair travels outside. That difference is where its double stops come from.

The bow outside

Erhu, banhu, jinghu, gaohu, zhonghu, gehu, zhuihu, yehu, sihu — in each, the hair passes between the strings and bears on both sides. Here it grazes them from outside, in one direction. The player must manage the angle; both strings can sound at once.

So it plays two pitches by nature. The sihu splits its hair in two, the erhu presses; the matouqin does nothing extra. Keeping the hair at a right angle to the string is its first rule of bowing.

Skin on both faces

The other oddity is the box: skin on the front and on the back. Other bowed strings skin only the front and leave a sound hole behind. Sealing both faces closes the sound into a smaller space, and what comes out is thicker and darker — half of that deep tone is the form.

The box is trapezoidal, framed in hardwood, once hollowed from one block and later built from four boards. The head is carved as a horse, galloping or standing. It is not ornament; it is where the name and legends come from.

Two strings, or one sheaf

Calling it two strings is inexact. Each is a bundle of dozens to over a hundred filaments: ninety nylon strands on top, a hundred and thirty below. Too many and it goes hard, too few and it goes soft; the count is a parameter to be tuned. The material has moved from horsehair to nylon to metal.

The common tuning is an inverted fourth, g–c¹, with the thinner string inside and the thicker outside; d¹–a and d¹–g also occur. A reform raised the tuning by a fourth, widening the range while keeping the soft core of the tone.

Three reforms, two forced by weather

Qi Baoligao, born in 1944, is a nationally recognised bearer of Mongolian matouqin music. In 1973 he was playing on the Xilingol steppe when rain came down; the herders sat still and listened while the moisture soaked the drum skin, and it went hoarse and then silent. He switched to python skin, which shrugs off damp: ten minutes with an orchestra before it drifts, where the old skin drifted in five.

In 1982 he had a paulownia face made. The first was too thick and a conductor said it sounded like an eighteen-yuan violin; thinned, the sound arrived that afternoon. White pine followed in 1996. The third reform was technique, not the instrument: violin, erhu, sihu and cello methods, plus new bow strokes.

  • 整个拉弦段唯一的例外 —— 「马头琴的定弦有多种，因较细的琴弦张在里弦，较粗的弦张在外弦，所以常以反四度关系定弦为 g · c¹，有时也以正四度关系定弦为 d¹ · a，正五度关系定弦为 d¹ · g」； 最关键的一句：「拉奏方法也与其它拉弦乐器不同，琴弓的弓毛不夹在里 · 外弦之间，而是在两弦外面擦奏，所以它具有独特的音色，柔和 · 浑厚而深沉，拉奏起来特别洪阔 · 低沉而豪放，富有草原风味」（北京乐器学会资料）—— 一句话就把马头琴从二胡、板胡、高胡、中胡、革胡、坠胡、椰胡、四胡全部分开了：那八件的弓毛都在两弦之间，只有它的弓在外面

  • 师承与身份：齐·宝力高（1944 年生 · 蒙古族 · 1944— ）为第二批国家级非物质文化遗产项目"蒙古族马头琴音乐"代表性传承人，1958 年考入内蒙古艺校（艺术学校）；「没有恩师桑都楞就没有我的今天」，16 岁在中央音乐学院学了小提琴（ 「我16 岁在中央音乐学院学了小提琴以后，发现马头琴是太落伍了。我也要改！」）依人民日报报道
