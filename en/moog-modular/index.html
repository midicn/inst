The Moog modular synthesizer

The East Coast keyboard philosophy — a wall of modules, and the baroque album that made the world accept the synthesizer

「1964 年，电气工程师兼音乐爱好者 Robert Moog 在纽约 AES 大会上发表论文『Voltage-Controlled Electronic Music Modules』」.

⭐⭐ The main line: two philosophies of two coasts

One era, one principle（voltage control）— and two utterly different instruments:

⭐ East Coast · Moog（1964）⭐ West Coast · Buchla（1963）
Interface⭐ keyboard⭐⭐ deliberately no keyboard（touch plates + sequencers）
Methodsubtractive（rich wave → filter carves）a new timbral language
Standard⭐ 1V/octave + pulse trigger（became the industry dialect）banana jacks · its own standards
Commissionerwith composer Herbert DeutschSan Francisco Tape Music Center（Subotnick · Sender）
Usersavant-garde to pop（Beatles · Monkees · Carlos · Emerson）Terry Riley · Steve Reich
Commerce⭐ enormous successfar less successful, equally innovative

⭐⭐ 「Buchla 设想的是一种新的音色语言，而非对原声乐器的电子模仿」— what the inventor plays, and who he builds for, decides the character of the instrument （compare the Martenot: a cellist built a singing instrument）.

⭐⭐ And Moog's reason for embracing the keyboard was practical: the keyboard is a ready-made 「control-voltage generator」 — one fixed voltage per key. The East Coast victory was half philosophy, half standardisation.

⭐⭐ The ladder filter: subtractive synthesis' prototype tool

「Moog 合成器的定义性创新是指数电压控制振荡器，以及尤其是压控低通滤波器—— 著名的『Moog 梯形滤波器』——它成为减法合成音色塑造的原型工具，至今对滤波器设计影响巨大」.

⭐⭐ The longest-lived part of an invention is often not the whole instrument but one part of it — every ladder-filter clone in Eurorack today descends from that circuit.

⭐⭐ Herbert Deutsch: the spark from a composer

「1963 年末，Moog 遇到了作曲家 Herbert Deutsch——他在 1964 年将电压控制振荡器和放大器模块与键盘相结合，设计出第一个原型」; 「与德国音乐家的合作激发了 Moog 扩展模块范围并将模块合并为整个系统的动力」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's invention genealogy: Hammond invented alone（a clockmaker）· Telharmonium alone（a lawyer）— the Moog modular is a co-authorship of engineer × composer. （⭐ user feedback shaped the product too: 「他总是喜欢从客户那里寻求反馈，为系列产品添加更多模块」.）

⭐⭐ An origin easter egg: nineteen-year-old Moog built theremins

「R. A. Moog 早在 19 岁就开始构造 Theremin」.

⭐⭐ Between the two grand masters of electronic instruments stands a theremin built by a teenager. （⭐ what he learned from it — how oscillators speak, what players need — grew into the synthesizer.）

⭐⭐ Switched-On Bach: the world accepts the synthesizer

「1968 年，作曲家 Wendy Carlos 在热门专辑 Switched-On Bach 里使用了这件乐器—— 第一张获得格莱美（三项）并卖出超百万张的合成器专辑—— 世界由此接受了 Moog 的发明可以用于高度音乐性与表现力的演奏」.

⭐⭐ One baroque album changed the instrument's fate: 「想要向 Carlos 的商业成功看齐，许多制片商、制作人和音乐家都购买了 Moog 模块化合成器」; 「1969 年，Moog 的工厂 42 名雇员每周制造两到三套完整的模块化系统」.

⭐ And it was 「最早的商业多轨录音之一」 — a landmark of recording history as well as synth history.

⭐⭐ Why Bach? — ⭐ counterpoint, precision, structure: exactly the proof-problem a monophonic, track-by-track instrument could solve — the least baroque instrument proving itself the most baroque.

⭐⭐ Where the word 「synthesizer」 comes from

「1929 年法国 Coupleux 与 Givelet 的乐器——第一次将乐器称为『合成器』」; 「该术语于 1956 年随 RCA Mark I 问世被正式引入」— 「RCA Mark I: 12 tuning forks excited electromagnetically… controlled by paper tape — continuous automation of pitch, volume, timbre and envelope — but spontaneous performance was impossible」.

⭐⭐ The RCA was a programmable-not-playable synthesizer; the Moog a playable one — that one step carried the synthesizer out of the institutional lab and onto the stage.

⭐⭐ Decline and revival

「1979 年复音预置合成器洪流（Prophet-5 · Jupiter-4）——第一代模块化的黄金时代落幕」; 「1983 年 MIDI 到来，模块化退入地下」; 「2010s：Eurorack 复兴」.

⭐⭐ A complete U-curve: birth → throne → underground → revival. ⭐ Keith Emerson's wall never vanished — it shrank（the Minimoog）and grew back（Eurorack）.

  • ⭐⭐ Switched-On Bach（1968）：让世界接受合成器 —— 「1968 年作曲家 Wendy Carlos 在她的热门专辑 Switched-On Bach 里使用了这件乐器——第一张获得格莱美（三项）并卖出超百万张的合成器专辑——世界由此接受了 Moog 的发明可以用于高度音乐性与表现力的演奏」；「想要把合成器新声音转变为资本并向 Carlos 看齐，许多制片商、制作人和音乐家都购买了 Moog 模块化合成器」；「1969 年，Moog 的工厂 42 名雇员每周制造两到三套完整的模块化系统」；⭐⭐ 「该录音还是最早的商业多轨录音之一」
