Prophet-5

The synthesizer learned to remember — forty patch slots and a Z80 microprocessor

Related

Voltage controlMinimoogARP 2600Moog modular synthesizerTimbrePitch

The previous page's synthesizer was still 「alive」 — every knob turn was one-off, its sound irreproducible.

This page, the synthesizer learned to remember.

「The Prophet-5 facilitated a move from synthesizers creating unpredictable sounds to producing 'a standard package of familiar sounds'.」

⭐⭐ This page is the Prophet-5 — fifth in the analog-synth family. One line: the synthesizer learned to remember.

⭐⭐ The main line: from unpredictable to a standard package

Before the Prophet-5:

「合成器需要用户调整旋钮来改变声音——无法保证精确复现一个声音」.

⭐⭐ Which meant live shows and studios were gambles: yesterday's sound could not be wound back into today's knob positions. （Compare Manzarek's frustration — in the modular era, 「reproduction」 meant hand-drawn patch diagrams.）

⭐⭐ The Prophet-5's fix:

「Sequential 用微处理器让用户即时召回声音而非手动重建」— 「facilitated a move from synthesizers creating unpredictable sounds to producing 'a standard package of familiar sounds'」.

⭐⭐ 「标准包」 sounds flat, but it is a revolution: from then on the synth could tour, could record, could change hands — because it remembered.

⭐⭐ The microprocessor enters the instrument

「当时 Smith 有一份与微处理器打交道的全职工作——一项新技术」; 「Smith 想到把它们与合成器芯片结合做成可编程合成器」.

⭐⭐ The architecture is one of 「old and new in one body」: the voice is still analog（VCO→filter→VCA）; the control is already digital（a Z80 scanning and storing every parameter） — the first true hybrid instrument.

⭐⭐ Against the site's other hybrids: the tonewheel organ（mechanical wheel + magnetic pickup）· the Prophet-5（analog voice + digital memory） — every 「old-new body」 has founded a new category.

⭐⭐ Dave Smith quits

「他起初没有 pursuing 这个想法，以为 Moog 或 ARP 会先设计出来。当没有乐器出现时，1977 年初，Smith 辞去工作全职投入」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's invention genealogy: the Minimoog — built behind the boss's back; the Moog modular — engineer × composer; the Prophet-5 — quit and committed. ⭐ And Smith later co-created MIDI（sharing a 2013 Technical Grammy with Roland's Kakehashi）— 「发明了现代合成器，不止一次」.

⭐⭐ The Prophet-10 overheats → halved into a classic

「起初 Smith 与 Bowen 开发的是 Prophet-10（十声部）——但它不稳定、迅速过热，产生音准问题；两人移除一半电子件，把声部减到五，造出 Prophet-5」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's other halvings: the Minimoog's 44 keys（not 88） · the Hammond's seven cut pedal notes · the Prophet-10 halved to five — 「subtracting」 is the hardware world's recurring sobriety.

⭐⭐ Three revisions and an endless argument

「Rev1 手工组装只 182 台（koa 木壳）→ Rev2 加磁带 patch 存储 · 核桃壳 → Rev3 换 Curtis CEM 芯片——『仍然 impressively 好弹，但比早期型号稍冷、稍无特征』」 ~6000 台.

⭐⭐ 「Which revision sounds best」 is argued among collectors to this day — swap a chip, change a character（compare the ladder-filter clone wars: the circuit is the identity）.

⭐⭐ Poly-Mod: analog FM hiding in the back room

「Poly-Mod 允许一个振荡器与滤波包络调制另一只振荡器的频率与脉宽」.

⭐⭐ 「模拟 FM」hidden inside a subtractive machine — two years later Yamaha's true FM synthesis（the DX7）would reset the whole industry （see the digital-synth entries）. ⭐ The Prophet-5 was that door, opened a crack early.

⭐⭐ The player lineage: from Thriller to Kid A

「Michael Jackson（Thriller 上大量使用）· Madonna · Phil Collins（In the Air Tonight）· The Cars · Kraftwerk（1981 巡演）· ABBA（The Visitors）· Radiohead（Kid A）」; 「Peter Gabriel 称它为他的『old warhorse』」.

⭐⭐ From 1982's《Thriller》to 2000's《Kid A》— eighteen years and two musical worlds, one synthesizer. ⭐ The real victory of the 「standard package」: it became the default sound-source of an entire era.

⭐⭐ The company: bankruptcy, buyback, farewell

「Sequential Circuits 1987 年破产」→ Yamaha → 「2002 年创立 Dave Smith Instruments，2015 年回购 Sequential 品牌名」→ 「2020 年 Prophet-5 Rev4 忠实复刻」→ 「Dave Smith 于 2022 年去世——可编程复音合成器与 MIDI，是他留给地球上每位音乐家的永久礼物」.

⭐⭐ From bankruptcy to buying the brand back — a forty-four-year circle. ⭐ 「发明了现代合成器，不止一次」 — no better epitaph on this site.

Before it, a synth's timbre was luck; after it, timbre was storage.

⭐⭐ 《写字歌》writes exactly this: writing the timbre down —

the score remembers the notes; the patch remembers the sound; the instrument finally has a memory of its own.
