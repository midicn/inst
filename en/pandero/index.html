Pandeiro

Like a tambourine, but not one — a tunable frame drum, the heartbeat of Brazilian rhythm

Related

TambourineBangu (Peking-opera drum)AnvilTimbreArticulation Marks

Fourteen-odd Western percussion instruments are done in this section, and most of them look different from one another.

Only this one looks almost exactly like another one — and is a different instrument.

「潘德罗与铃鼓外观相似……但相似到此为止。」

⭐⭐ This page is the pandeiro — the frame drum of Brazil. One line: like a tambourine, but not one.

⭐⭐ The main line: five differences that take the 「like」 apart

How alike are they? Same family, same outline, same ring of jingles. Put them side by side and five differences hold:

⭐ pandeirotambourine
① How it is held⭐⭐ horizontal（head to the sky）vertical
② Head tension⭐⭐ tunable — 「允许演奏者在高低音之间选择」fixed
③ The head's voice⭐⭐ the skin sound matters enormouslybarely matters（the jingles lead）
④ The skinremovable（swap and tune）fixed
⑤ The jinglesdrier, shorter sustain（cupped platinelas）more sustained「沙」

⭐⭐ Row three is half the story: the tambourine's sound lives in its jingles; the pandeiro's lives in the skin — it is a drum first, with jingles attached.

⭐⭐ The verdict: 「潘德罗可以说是两者中更万能的」— 「潘德罗多用于巴西音乐，蒙皮铃鼓多用于福音与古典」.

⭐⭐ One drum, four strokes, a complete groove

A tambourine in an orchestra mostly shakes and taps. The pandeiro does not:

「典型潘德罗节奏由拇指、指尖、掌根与手掌交替奏出。」

⭐⭐ Thumb, fingertips, heel of the hand, palm — four contacts, four timbres, rotating on one skin: one player is a complete rhythm section（bass from the heel pressed into the head, snap from the fingertips）.

⭐⭐ Two modern additions: 「还可以摇动发声，或用手指在鼓面上搓出一种『锉』声」.

⭐ The thumb roll is shared with the tambourine — 「拇指在钹片上快速摩擦产生滚音，需要大量练习找到正确的压力」.

⭐⭐ Two instruments, one name: the square one is five hundred years older

The plan files the pandeiro under 「early/historical instruments」 — with reason, because there used to be two of them:

⭐ medieval pandeiro（Adufe）⭐ Brazilian pandeiro
Shapesquareround
Headsdouble-skinned（often a bell inside the frame）single
HomeIberia（still played in Portuguese and Spanish villages）Brazil
Eraseen in 13th-century illustrationsmodern

⭐⭐ 「这是潘德罗的原型，南美鼓是它更现代的后裔」— it derives from a Moorish/Arabic instrument; smaller versions survive in North Africa.

⭐⭐ Same family as the saer's 「同名异实」: the Qing 「塞他尔」 was plucked, today's is bowed; the medieval 「潘德罗」 was square and double-skinned, today's is round and single — the name lived on for centuries while the object changed its body.

⭐⭐ The route: a mirror of the Chinese instruments

The bili entered China westward（Kucha → Liangzhou temples → Central Plains folk）; the pandeiro walked the other half of the same Moorish route:

「潘德罗源自中东的框鼓，经北非、欧洲与葡萄牙殖民者到达巴西」; the Brazilian pandeiro descends from the pandeireta / pandereta of Spain and Portugal.

⭐⭐ military band → temple → folk（bili）; Moor → Iberia → colonists → Brazil（pandeiro）. Opposite directions, same structure: once an instrument enters the rhythmic life of a civilization, it never leaves.

⭐⭐ In Brazil: the unofficial national instrument

「潘德罗流行于巴西，甚至被称为那个国家的非官方乐器」.

The grooves it carries: samba · choro · baião · frevo · capoeira — ⭐ 「潘德罗最大的用处大概仍在桑巴与 choro」.

⭐⭐ 「现代潘德罗之父」: Marcos Suzano 「发现了新的演奏技法，把潘德罗应用于 funk、摇滚与电子等各种音乐风格」— a folk frame drum pushed into the rhythm section of electronic music.

⭐ Early pioneers: Jackson do Pandeiro · João da Baiana; choro master Jorginho do Pandeiro.

⭐⭐ Skin vs plastic: a practical fork

The instrument is still made by hand and by factory, and the trade-offs are concrete:

⭐ natural skin（goat）plastic head
Origin⭐ hand-made（artesanal）factory
⭐⭐ Exclusive⭐⭐ real bass tones（impossible on plastic）stable under high tension
⭐ Weightas light as possible（the holding hand tires fast）much heavier
Repertoirechorosamba & pagode

⭐⭐ 「许多场合两者同时演奏，互为支撑」.

⭐⭐ Two 「improper」 usages

Jazz and rock drummers picked the pandeiro up, and it grew new seats:

Stanton Moore — 「把潘德罗调低音、发出像落地桶鼓带钹片的声音，装上架子并整合进现代鼓组」;

Sule Greg Wilson（Carolina Chocolate Drops, the Grammy-winning《Genuine Negro Jig》）— 「把它与一只可调的博德兰鼓（也装架）当一对用刷子演奏，做出鼓组效果」.

⭐⭐ A frame drum from 13th-century Iberia found a new seat in a 21st-century drum kit.

  • ⭐ 传播路径（与中国乐器的丝路经历互为镜像）：「潘德罗源自中东的框鼓，经北非、欧洲与葡萄牙殖民者到达巴西」；巴西潘德罗源自西班牙葡萄牙的 pandeireta / pandereta依专题类资料 —— ⭐⭐ 同一条摩尔→伊比利亚→殖民航线，把一件框鼓送进了另一个大陆的节奏心脏（与筚篥的「军乐队→寺院→民间」路径方向相反、结构相同）

  • ⭐⭐ 形制与选材（两面皮 vs 塑料皮的取舍）：「天然皮潘德罗常被称为手工（artesanal）乐器，塑料皮潘德罗多为工厂制造」；⭐⭐ 天然皮的独门优势：「可以打出漂亮的低音，塑料皮做不到」；⭐ 重量差别是实用大事：「塑料头的潘德罗重得多（需要坚固结构支撑高张力），天然皮潘德罗尽可能轻以提升演奏舒适度——持鼓手拿重乐器疲劳很快，不重视甚至会导致受伤」；⭐ 分工：「天然皮用于 choro，塑料头更适合桑巴与 pagode；许多场合两者同时演奏，互为支撑」依专题类资料
