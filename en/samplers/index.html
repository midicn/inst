Samplers

Recording the world as an instrument — pen and thesaurus

The previous page's DX7 computed its sound — this page's instrument says: don't compute, record the world.

⭐⭐ This page is the samplers — second of the digital-synth family. One line: record the world into an instrument.

⭐⭐ The main line: record the world into an instrument

「Fairlight CMI……是世界上第一台数字采样合成器」— 「最初的 CMI 创造时并没有明确它该用于哪种合成。在写加法合成应用失败后， Vogel 的团队决定实验用其它乐器的数字化采样作为起点。这成功了， CMI 以第一台实用采样器重生」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's unplanned inventions: the theremin from an alarm · the xun from a hunting tool · the TB-303 from failure — ⭐⭐ Fairlight's sampling was also a pivot after a plan failed. And this pivot widened the definition of 「instrument」: anything you recorded was one.

⭐⭐ Page R: the ancestor of every DAW

「它的 Page R 音序器，设定了今天大多数音序软件遵循的模式」; 「被制作人重度使用——把 CMI 装满鼓采样、有效地当作鼓机用」.

⭐⭐ Page 6 was the waveform drawing page（draw with a light pen）· Page 8 the sampling page · Page 9 the keyboard sequencer page — the very concept of 「Pages」 is the direct ancestor of today's DAWs.

⭐⭐ Against the site's 「first screens」: the Martenot's intensity key handed 「dynamics」 to the hand; Fairlight's light pen handed 「visible sound」 to it — before MIDI and the mouse, someone saw sound and changed it by hand for the first time.

⭐⭐ The light pen and the green phosphor screen

「即使是附带的绿磷光单色视频显示器，带着它的光笔界面，也成了那个时代音乐的图标」.

⭐⭐ The folk mirrors: 《绣花灯》 — embroidering waveforms on a screen with a light pen; 《逛灯》 — the lamp being strolled was the first screen that let you see sound.

⭐⭐ $25,000 aristocrats vs the Emulator's commoners

⭐⭐ The Emulator's name came from 「翻同义词词典」 — 「『Emulator』这个名字来自翻同义词词典的结果——与公司名完美匹配」— ⭐ the name of a billion-dollar industry, found in a thesaurus.

⭐⭐ Against the polysynth page: E-mu's Dave Rossum — collaborator on the Oberheim Poly and the Prophet-5 — became Fairlight's rival in the sampling era. The same hidden river, a third time.

⭐⭐ Trevor Horn: 「the man who invented the eighties」

「Horn，被认为『发明了八十年代的人』，第一次在他项目 The Buggles 的第二张专辑 Adventures in Modern Recording 上使用他著名的采样技法」; 「这些采样技法后来用于 Horn 制作的唱片：Grace Jones 的 Slave to the Rhythm · Art of Noise 的 The Seduction of Claude Debussy · Frankie Goes To Hollywood 的 Welcome to the Pleasuredome」.

⭐⭐ One instrument + one producer's techniques = the pop sound of a decade. （⭐ against Dave Smith 「发明了现代合成器，不止一次」 — Horn was the inventor by usage.）

⭐⭐ The SP-1200: 12-bit grit as the sound of hip-hop

「E-mu SP-1200（1987）曾是且仍是 hip-hop 制作中最受推崇的采样器之一。它的 12-bit 采样引擎给乐器一种可取的温暖、给鼓一种砂砾感」.

⭐⭐ Against the site's third recording: the Hammond key click · guitar feedback · the Minimoog's drift · the 960's drift — this time 「low resolution」 itself became a genre's voice: hip-hop's sound archaeology begins at 12-bit.

⭐⭐ Fairlight's bankruptcy: the end of the aristocratic road

「1985 年，市面上的其它采样器在价格点上打压 CMI」; 「CMI 的处理架构对采样这件事其实不够高效，E-mu Emulator 与 Ensoniq Mirage 等键盘以零头价格做基本采样」; 「原 Fairlight 公司 1986 年破产」.

⭐⭐ Against the Prophet-5's company history（bankruptcy → buyback）and Moog（selling the company）— the pioneer's fate is often to watch their invention become affordable.

Before the sampler, an instrument was something you built; after it, an instrument was something you recorded.

⭐⭐ The world's sounds became collectable into a keyboard for the first time —

from Fairlight's light pen to the SP-1200's grit, sampling became music's next grammar.
