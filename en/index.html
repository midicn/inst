How to read an instrument

Let structure explain the sound, then use the sound to check the structure.

  1. 01See the shapeMaterial, size and resonator decide what it can produce.
  2. 02Check the rangeThe range chart shows where it sits in an ensemble.
  3. 03Hear the timbreCompare within the family: the difference is usually attack and decay, not pitch.
  4. 04Trace its useWhere an instrument is heard decides its repertoire.
