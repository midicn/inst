Vocal health and voice care

The only instrument on this site with a warranty — and the only one whose failure is also a failure of personhood

Teachers and singers sit in the same clinic

In the corridor of an ENT clinic the woman ahead is a primary-school teacher and the man behind her has just come off tour. They have the same complaint: nodules or polyps, with the cause written as improper or excessive voice use. The voice is the only instrument that goes to hospital when it breaks, and the only one whose repair decides whether you can keep working.

Three ways to damage it, three treatments

Voice medicine sorts the problem into three. Shouting is abuse: the right action, too much of it. Speaking with a locked throat is misuse: the action itself is wrong. Twenty years of singing is overuse: right action, too long. The treatments differ: misuse is corrected by training, abuse by cutting down, overuse by rest and scheduling. Voice therapy comes first because most voice disorders are rooted in habit, not in the folds.

Surgery removes the nodule, not the habit

Laser excision through a suspension laryngoscope succeeds often, but two rules matter more: the outcome tracks how well the cause is removed, and the condition recurs easily. After surgery come two weeks of silence, communicating by writing, then retraining once the wound heals. If the cause remains, recurrence is a matter of time. Diagnosis depends on stroboscopy, and the terminology is still unsettled; a field that cannot agree on the name of the lesion is no place for self-medication.

Three small things, and one impossible one

Throat-clearing is the most damaging everyday action, because the folds snap shut. Do not perform or teach on a full stomach; reflux is one of the causes, so leave two hours. Do not drink anything cold straight after singing, when the mucosa is already engorged. Puberty, menstruation and pregnancy leave the tissue tender and slow to recover. The last point has no fix: you cannot put your throat in a case the way you put away a guitar. Other instruments go to the repair shop; the voice goes to hospital, and it is also the thing you speak with.
