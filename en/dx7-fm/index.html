The DX7 and FM synthesis

No instrument owned a decade so completely — digital won, presets ruled

⭐⭐ This page is the DX7 and FM synthesis — first of the digital-synth family. One line: digital won, presets ruled.

⭐⭐ The main line: digital won, presets ruled

「DX7 是第一台成功的数字合成器，也是史上最畅销的合成器之一——卖出超过 200,000 台」.

⭐⭐ 「没有乐器如此完全地拥有一个十年。」 — against the Prophet-5's 「every polysynth lives in its shadow」: the DX7's shadow is the entire eighties of pop.

⭐⭐ Two firsts: ① digital won on character, not just convenience（a decade before software）; ② presets are not cheating — they are distribution: the mechanism by which a sound conquers a culture.

⭐⭐ FM's origin: a theory rejected by America

「FM 合成由 John Chowning 在斯坦福大学开发； 斯坦福申请专利并希望授权，但被 Hammond 与 Wurlitzer 等美国公司拒绝—— Chowning 觉得他们的工程师不懂 FM」.

⭐⭐ Then the page's most dramatic contrast:

「Yamaha 的一位首席工程师访问斯坦福。据 Chowning 说：『十分钟他就懂了……我猜 Yamaha 已在数字领域工作，所以他完全明白我在说什么。』」

⭐⭐ One technology — 「unintelligible」 on one side, 「understood in ten minutes」 on the other. The ones who understood were not smarter; they were already living in that world. ⭐ Against the Telharmonium killed by crosstalk: a technology's fate often depends on 「who is in the room」.

⭐⭐ GS1 → DX7: the victory of two chips

「第一台商用 FM 合成器是 Yamaha GS1（1980），因集成电路芯片而制造昂贵」; 「Yamaha 同时在开发 VLSI 芯片——这让 DX7 只用两颗芯片，对照 GS1 的 50 颗」; 「$1,995 · 16 声部复音 · 力度+触后键盘」.

⭐⭐ 「50 颗芯片 → 2 颗芯片」— democratisation is not a price cut; it is the machine getting smaller. （⭐ against the Prophet-5's Z80「remembering」timbres — the DX7's VLSI「computing」them.）

⭐⭐ FM's character: moments, not sustains

「对照模拟合成器的『温暖』『模糊』——DX7 声音『刺耳』『玻璃质』『冰冷』，更丰富更亮」; 「它以电钢琴、钟铃与其它『敲击』『拨弦』的声音著称——强调复杂的起音瞬态，当时大多数模拟合成器无法产生」.

⭐⭐ Subtractive synthesis does 「sustains」（filters sweeping slowly）; FM does 「moments」（the instant of a strike, computed whole） — the real fork between the two synthesis philosophies.

⭐⭐ 32 algorithms: FM's wiring diagrams

「音调生成基于 FM 合成：使用多只正弦波振荡器，能以 32 种『算法』提供的各种配置互相调制——从而生成丰富多样的谐波与非谐波频谱」.

⭐⭐ Against subtractive synthesis's 「one signal chain」: FM's 「algorithms」 are 32 wiring diagrams — each one a different instrument topology.

⭐⭐ And one thing a subtractive synth has that the DX7 does not: a filter. FM needs no filter — the spectrum is computed, not filtered.

⭐⭐ The decade of presets: the E.PIANO 1 dynasty

「FM 编程通过膜面板与两行显示屏出了名地痛苦，所以几乎没人做。相反，世界靠预设运转，尤其是 E.PIANO 1——那个在录音里有效替代 Rhodes 的电钢琴 patch」; 「它在 Whitney Houston 抒情曲、Chicago 的《19》、无数 quiet-storm 与福音唱片上」.

⭐⭐ 「1986 年，它被用于美国 Billboard Hot 100 冠军单曲的 40%」.

⭐⭐ Forty percent — the market share of one factory preset. ⭐ Against the Prophet-5（patch memory lets you store 「your own」）— the DX7's presets let everyone 「use the same one」. That is what 「presets are distribution」 means: timbre was copied like a record for the first time.

⭐⭐ Interfaces decide history

「接口决定历史：DX7 的不可编程性正是模拟以 knobs-per-function 设计（如复刻的 MS-20）强势回归的原因」.

⭐⭐ Three lessons（the page's hardest passage）:

「① 数字可以在性格上获胜，而不只是便利——比软件早十年； ② 预设不是作弊；它们是分发——一个声音征服一种文化的机制（后来把 stock 插件送进排行榜音乐的同一机制）； ③ 接口决定历史。」

⭐⭐ The YM2612 enters the games console

「基于 DX7 声音芯片的芯片，如 YM2612，被用于 Sega Genesis 游戏机等技术」.

⭐⭐ FM's 「glassiness」 became the voice of a generation of consoles — the first meeting of electronic-instrument history and games-console history.

⭐⭐ Two extreme users: Eno and Enya

「Brian Eno 熟练编程自己的音色，它对他的氛围音乐作品至关重要」; 「Tubular Bells 预设 essentially 是新一代音乐人的首选武器，最著名用于 Enya 的首张专辑」.

⭐⭐ Two extremes of one machine: Eno treated FM as a language（he programmed）; Enya as furniture（she used presets）— 「presets are distribution」 and 「programming is creation」 were both true on the DX7.
