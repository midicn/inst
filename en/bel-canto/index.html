Bel canto

The name came thirty years after the singing died — a tradition's name is its epitaph

Related

⭐⭐ The main line: the name came thirty years after the singing died

「The phrase was not associated with a 『school』 of singing until the early 1860s, when writers used it nostalgically to describe a manner of singing that had begun to wane around 1830」; Rossini's lament: 「Alas for us, we have lost our bel canto.」

⭐⭐ The tradition died first, the name came after — bel canto is its own epitaph. (Against the site's name-vs-reality lineage: the talk box's three claimants · the rhythm machine's name arriving fifteen years late — bel canto goes further: the name was born when its object no longer existed.)

⭐⭐ The covered technique: technology replacing surgery

⭐⭐ 「male voices solved the great obstacle of the register transition through the covered (masked) technique, gaining a bright, solid chest high register」; women's voices sought roundness, connection and flexibility, shifting at will between sustained singing and leaps, slides and trills」. 「Composers wrote demanding arias to display this art of singing — they greatly expanded the possibilities of natural human singing, without resorting to inhuman physiological modification」.

⭐⭐ The second golden age's essence: technology replacing surgery — against the children-voice entry: humanity finally found the answer that does not cut the body (what the castrati wanted was high notes plus adult lungs; bel canto's covered technique took them from ordinary male voices).

⭐⭐ The lit-candle test

「an exercise said to demonstrate its epitome involves a singer holding a lit candle to her mouth and singing without causing the flame to flicker」.

⭐⭐ Bel canto's final exam is 「do not blow out the candle」 — a technical system that proves itself by zero disturbance. (Against the talk box's plastic tube: one filters with a mouth that must not move, the other with a mouth that must — two extreme mouths, two definitions of beauty.)

⭐⭐ An almost lost performance system

⭐⭐ Nineteenth-century bel canto was not 「beauty」 but a whole engineering of 「how to speak one line of text」:

TechniqueWhat it governs
Prosodic singing「speaking」 the words through accent and emphasis
Register matchingmatching voice register and colour to the words' emotion
Articulated phrasingphrasing built on grammatical and rhetorical pauses
Portamento (several kinds)the posture of the slide between notes
Messa di vocecrescendo–decrescendo on one pitch — the principal expressive source
Rhythmic rubatofrequent temporal give-and-take
Graces & divisionsthe variety of ornaments and divisions
Gesturethe body as a tool of delivery

⭐⭐ Vibrato was once punctuation, later a ground: 「singers reserved it for heightening the expression of certain words……rather than as a continuous component of tone」 — one parameter moving from rhetorical tool to physical ground: a two-hundred-year aesthetic shift.

⭐⭐ The eclipse: a man who wanted legato was sung as declamation

「Wagner……intended for those voices to retain the bel canto approach; to sing his operas as one would sing Bellini's Norma. However, the tradition at Bayreuth was to declaim, and because the public demanded it, the singers complied」.

⭐⭐ Even Wagner's intent was betrayed by the Bayreuth tradition — bel canto died at the hands of its own disciples. 「tenors began delivering high notes from the chest rather than a head voice or falsetto, sacrificing agility」 — high notes became local instead of borrowed, and flexibility died.

⭐⭐ Potter: the loss of castrato skills manufactured the myth of bel canto — 「the loss of their irrecoverable skills in time created the myth of bel canto」 — the extinction of an institution manufactures a tradition's golden memory.

⭐ The 1950s: Callas's archaeology

⭐⭐ 「interpreters and teachers re-examined early 19th-century scores and performance methods」 — Callas · Sutherland · Caballé · Horne · Sills; tenors: Kraus · Flórez · Pavarotti. ⭐⭐ A tradition revives by re-reading the archive.

⭐⭐ 「singers can use a bel canto method of singing even in verismo, Wagnerian, Verdian, and modern styles」 — ⭐⭐ bel canto died as a 「period style」 and lives as a 「technical system」: it is now the ground of every vocal style — a language no longer named after itself.
